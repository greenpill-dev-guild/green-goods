# Research agenda

Applies the research agenda edits ticked in the Linear document *Research agenda · pending edits*, runs the delegated review of the unticked ones at each Research cycle boundary, and promotes a confirmed candidate track into `docs/routines/research-agenda.md`. The weekly `research-synthesis` routine proposes; this skill is the hand that applies, so the agenda stays human-owned. The delegated review accepts evidence appends, anchor identifier updates and answered questions by rule, rejects edits whose anchor is gone or that only restate Linear state, and puts every change to a track's stage, horizon, order, questions or bars to the steward.

**When to use it:** At a Research cycle boundary (`review`), or whenever the Saturday research digest says accepted edits are waiting (`apply`).

**What you get:** The agenda file updated with exactly the accepted edits, the edition bumped once with a change-log line, and the document's lines moved to Applied or Rejected; a review also leaves a dated *Delegated review* section in the document. The commit stays yours, or the session's after a review, and the steward approves the diff.

**How to invoke:** Type `/research-agenda`, or `/research-agenda review` at a cycle boundary, or `/research-agenda candidates <name>` after the panel confirms a candidate track.

[`SKILL.md`](./SKILL.md) in this folder is the executable contract; this README is the human
summary the [Skills Catalog](https://docs.greengoods.app/builders/agentic/skills) projects.
