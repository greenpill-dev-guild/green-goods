# Hats Protocol

[Hats Protocol](https://docs.hatsprotocol.xyz) provides the on-chain role hierarchy that decides
who may do what in a garden. Gardeners submit work, evaluators attest assessments, and stewards
approve work. Roles stack, so stewards can also do what the roles below them do, and the garden's
owner can do all of it. Because hats are tokens, the
[EAS resolvers](./eas) check them directly at write time, and because each garden's hats form a
tree, its whole authority structure is inspectable at once.

## How it flows

Stewards and the garden's owner grant and revoke roles through the Hats module. A grant reaches
down the tree: the owner role brings the steward and gardener hats with it, and the steward role
brings the evaluator and gardener hats. A revoke does not: it removes the one hat it names, so
taking someone out of a garden means revoking each hat they hold.

One role can also be self-served: while a garden has open joining on, anyone who is not yet a
member can call `joinGarden` on the garden account and receives the gardener hat. A steward or the
owner turns that on or off with `setOpenJoining` and can limit it with `setMaxGardeners`. That
limit counts successful `joinGarden` calls, not current members: a gardener hat granted at mint or
by a steward does not count toward it, and revoking a hat does not free a place.

One more caller can write the tree. The module accepts a call that comes from the garden account
itself, and the account executes what the holder of the garden's token tells it to. So whoever
holds the token can grant and revoke roles through the account without wearing a hat, which is why
[handing a garden over](./tokenbound) means moving the token and the hats together. Everything else
only reads the tree.

```mermaid
flowchart LR
  steward([Owner or steward]) -->|grants or revokes| module["Hats module"]
  joiner([Anyone, while open joining is on]) -->|joinGarden| account["Garden account"]
  holder([Garden token holder]) -->|executes a role call| account
  account -->|grants the gardener hat, or relays the holder's call| module
  module --> tree["Garden hat tree<br/>(Hats Protocol)"]
  resolvers["EAS resolvers"] -->|check the signer's hat| tree
  apps["Client and admin"] -->|read roles| tree
```

## A gotcha worth knowing early

Hats has no on-chain wearer enumeration, and revoking a hat is a transfer. Never build a guard on
hat supply; when you need "who wears this hat", the answer comes from
[the Hats subgraph](https://docs.hatsprotocol.xyz/for-developers/v1-sdk/subgraph), not a contract
call.

## Working with it locally

The [fork mode](../getting-started#other-modes) (`bun run dev -- fork`) runs a local fork of
Arbitrum One, so the Hats contract and every existing garden tree are available locally without
real gas.
