# Personas and Surfaces

Green Goods has five personas. Each one is tied to an on-chain hat, which is how the contracts know who may do what, and to the surfaces where its work happens. The ontology owns what a persona means; the route definitions own the paths it navigates. Read this page before the work-submission trace, which names these actors at every step.

## Personas

| Persona | Hat | Surfaces | Definition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gardener | `gardener` | admin, client, agent, public, docs | A person doing regenerative Work in a garden. Submits Work, holds gardener Hats, receives credit toward Hypercerts and yield splits. |
| Steward | `steward` | admin, client, agent, public, docs | A person who stewards a garden — creates Actions, approves Work, configures Vault and Hat hierarchy. Holds the garden's steward Hats (the deployed Operator role). |
| Evaluator | `evaluator` | admin, client, public, docs | A person who authors or strengthens Assessments and attests impact methodology or outcomes. Evaluators do not approve or reject submitted Work; that authority belongs to Stewards. |
| Funder | `funder` | admin, client, public, docs | A person or org who deposits into a garden's Vault, holds Hypercert fractions, receives yield distributions per configured splits. |
| Community Member | `community` | client, public, docs | A local resident affected by a Garden's Work. Signals or attests that Work exists and is healthy, and helps prioritize future Actions through public signal and conviction flows. |

## Where each persona works

**Gardener** holds the `gardener` hat. A person doing regenerative Work in a garden. Submits Work, holds gardener Hats, receives credit toward Hypercerts and yield splits. This persona works in the admin cockpit, the client app, the messaging agent, the public site and these docs.

**Steward** holds the `steward` hat. A person who stewards a garden — creates Actions, approves Work, configures Vault and Hat hierarchy. Holds the garden's steward Hats (the deployed Operator role). This persona works in the admin cockpit, the client app, the messaging agent, the public site and these docs.

**Evaluator** holds the `evaluator` hat. A person who authors or strengthens Assessments and attests impact methodology or outcomes. Evaluators do not approve or reject submitted Work; that authority belongs to Stewards. This persona works in the admin cockpit, the client app, the public site and these docs.

**Funder** holds the `funder` hat. A person or org who deposits into a garden's Vault, holds Hypercert fractions, receives yield distributions per configured splits. This persona works in the admin cockpit, the client app, the public site and these docs.

**Community Member** holds the `community` hat. A local resident affected by a Garden's Work. Signals or attests that Work exists and is healthy, and helps prioritize future Actions through public signal and conviction flows. This persona works in the client app, the public site and these docs.

The on-chain hat tree has six roles; the Owner hat carries full garden control but has no dedicated persona — owners act through the Steward surfaces.
