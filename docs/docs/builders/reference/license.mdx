is. In practice, you may use, copy, modify, merge, publish, distribute, sublicense, and sell
copies of the source, in commercial or private projects, on two conditions: include the copyright
and permission notice in all copies or substantial portions of the software, and accept that it
comes as-is, with no warranty.

The canonical text lives at
[`LICENSE`](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/blob/develop/LICENSE) in the
repository root: copyright 2024 to 2026, Greenpill Dev Guild.

A few practical notes for builders:

- **Forking and building on Green Goods is encouraged.** The [architecture](/builders/architecture)
  is deliberately composable, and the [design principles](/builders/architecture/design) treat
  community capability as the deliverable.
- **The license covers this repository's code.** Integrated protocols carry their own licenses
  (linked from each [integration page](/builders/integrations)), and deployed contract instances,
  live services, and the Green Goods name are operational matters rather than license grants.
