# Endow a Garden

An endowment is a deposit, not a gift. Your money stays yours and you can take it back out. While it sits there, it earns, and those earnings go to the garden. It is a way to support a garden for as long as you leave the deposit in place.

Read the next section before you deposit. An endowment carries risks a donation does not.

## What you are agreeing to

- **Where the money sits.** Your deposit goes into the garden's endowment, which lends it out through a lending market outside Green Goods to earn interest.
- **Where the earnings go.** Not to you. When a steward of the garden collects them, they are split: part goes to the garden's shared fund, part is set aside to fund the garden's impact certificates, and a small part goes to the Green Goods protocol.
- **Your deposit is not guaranteed.** If the lending market loses money, your share of the loss comes out of your deposit. You can get back less than you put in.
- **Withdrawals depend on what is available.** There is no lock-in and no notice period. If the lending market is short of funds when you ask, you may only be able to take part of it out, or have to try again later.
- **The token's price is your risk.** A deposit in WETH is worth more or fewer dollars as the price of ETH moves.
- **The software around it can change.** Green Goods can upgrade the contracts that manage endowments and route their earnings.

If any of that is not acceptable, [donate](/community/funder-guide/donating-to-a-garden), or do not give.

## Before you start

- **A crypto wallet,** with **DAI or WETH on the Arbitrum network** and a little ETH there for the network fee. The page does not take cards, and it does not convert other tokens for you.
- **A garden you want to support.** Read its approved work first, on its page at [greengoods.app/gardens](https://greengoods.app/gardens).
- **Use a wallet you will keep.** Your endowment belongs to the wallet that made it. You need the same wallet to take it back out.

## Steps

1. Open [greengoods.app/fund](https://greengoods.app/fund).
2. Under **Find a Garden to support**, find the garden.
3. Tap **Endow** on its row. A window opens titled **Endow to**, with the garden's name.
4. Enter the **Amount**, in dollars.
5. Under **Payment currency**, choose the token to deposit, usually **DAI** or **WETH**.
6. Tap **Connect Wallet** if your wallet is not connected yet.
7. Tap the button that reads **Endow**, your amount, and the token, then confirm in your wallet. The first time you use a token, your wallet asks twice: once to allow the token to be spent, and once for the deposit itself.

## How you know it worked

- The window says **Endowed**, with your amount and the garden's name.
- Your wallet's history shows the transaction on Arbitrum.
- On the Fund page, **Manage Endowments** now lists your deposit. It can take a moment to appear.

## What happens next

Your deposit is in place, and it is yours.

- **The garden's side.** Earnings build up over time. They reach the garden only when one of its stewards or owners collects them. Nothing does this automatically, so the timing is up to the garden.
- **Your side.** You can check your deposit, and take some or all of it out, from **Manage Endowments**. See [Remove an Endowment](/community/funder-guide/withdraw-from-a-vault).

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **This Garden has no endowment fund configured yet** | This garden cannot take endowments here yet. Choose another garden, or [donate](/community/funder-guide/donating-to-a-garden) |
| **We couldn't load payment options for this Garden** | A temporary problem. Close the window and try again |
| **The live ETH price isn't reaching us on this network** | The page cannot price WETH right now. Choose **DAI**, or try later |
| A message says the endowment is not accepting deposits right now | Deposits are paused for this garden. Try another garden, or ask the garden |
| **Transaction cancelled** | You closed the wallet's confirmation. Nothing was deposited |
| **Transaction failed** | Read the message under it. The usual causes are not enough of the token, or not enough ETH for the fee |
| Your deposit is not listed under **Manage Endowments** | Check that you connected the wallet you deposited from, then give the page a moment to catch up |

For anything else, ask in the [Green Goods Telegram group](https://t.me/+N3o3_43iRec1Y2Jh).

:::note Not a charitable gift or an investment
An endowment through Green Goods is not tax-deductible, and no charity or nonprofit stands behind it. It is also not an investment: it is not meant to earn anything for you. This page is not financial advice.
:::
