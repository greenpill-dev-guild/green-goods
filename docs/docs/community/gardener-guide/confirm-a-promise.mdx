# Confirm a Promise

Confirm that the agreed help happened after checking its evidence. This is separate from approving a work submission or making a payment.

</figure>


## Before you start

- Sign in with the account that joined this garden. Visitors can read records; taking part requires membership.
- Check that the garden has an open pool. New money rewards and promise payouts are deferred.
- Photos and details can become public before you send the work record. Use your own evidence and read the [privacy guidance](/faq#is-what-i-upload-private).
- Be an eligible confirmer named by the agreement. The provider and contributing team cannot confirm their own promise, even through a steward fallback.
- Check the required confirmation count. Your confirmation cannot be taken back.

## Steps

1. The eligible confirmer opens the promise and checks the evidence. The action is **Confirm It Was Kept**; the review may ask **Did the help arrive?** or **Was the work you asked for done?**
2. Your confirmation cannot be taken back, and it may be only part of a group's required total. Read the confirmation count and check the evidence before choosing the button shown: **Confirm It Was Kept**, **Confirm the Help Arrived**, or **Confirm the Work Was Done**.
3. If the help is not ready to be called kept, choose **Not Yet** when offered. Write what still needs doing, then choose **Send to the Stewards** while online. Once it arrives, the promise reads **Under review by stewards** and ordinary confirmation stops until the review is resolved. It does not cancel the promise.

Opening **Not Yet** or saving its explanation does not send it. If sending fails, the explanation stays on this phone and the promise stays ready to confirm. Read its current status before using **Try Again**; ask your steward if the outcome is uncertain.

## How you know it worked

The confirmation count increases once your decision reaches the shared record. The promise reads **Kept** once the required total is met.

## What happens next

If more confirmations are required, the remaining eligible people act next. A successful **Not Yet** send moves the promise to **Under review by stewards**; ordinary confirmation pauses until that review is resolved.

## If something goes wrong

If no confirmation action appears, check whose turn it is, your account, team membership and the current state. Opening or saving a **Not Yet** explanation does not send it. After a failed send, keep the explanation on this phone and check the record before **Try Again**.

After an uncertain confirmation, check the count and history before acting again. A confirmation that already reached the record cannot be taken back. See [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync) for saved explanations and other queued actions.

Ask your steward about the agreement or record. For an app problem, open **Profile → Help**.
