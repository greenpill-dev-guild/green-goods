# Track Your Work

**Your Work** shows what you have recorded for the garden selected on Home: what is still on your phone, what is with your steward, and what they decided. This page explains each status, what approval means, and how rewards differ from approval.

## Before you start

- These navigation steps follow the current beta. Use the version your steward asked you to use; [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating) explains choosing the beta.
- Open the installed app, signed in to the account you submit with.
- You do not need a signal to look. The list shows what was saved the last time you were online.


## Steps

### 1. Open Your Work

1. Open **Home** and check the garden named at the top. Tap its name to choose another in **Your Gardens** if needed.
2. Tap the **Your Work** button at the top. It shows a number while anything is still on your phone.

There are two tabs. **Pending** holds everything that is not decided yet. **Completed** holds work a steward has reviewed.

### 2. Read the status

On the **Pending** tab, each item carries a label.

| Label | What it means and who acts next |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Saved on your phone, unfinished. Tap it to continue |
| **To upload** | The work record is ready to send. Tap **Upload all** when you have a signal |
| **Checking** | The app is checking whether the record arrived. Wait; do not submit a duplicate |
| **Can't upload** | The record cannot be sent as it is. Open it to see why |
| **Didn't upload** | A try failed. Open it and tap **Upload now** |
| **In review** | The record arrived. A steward acts next |

**Checking** means the outcome is still uncertain; do not assume the work failed or submit a duplicate. **In review** means your steward can review it. [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync) covers each of those labels.

These labels describe the work record. Queued photos and details may already have been prepared in public storage before the record is sent. [The privacy answers](/faq#is-what-i-upload-private) explain what becomes public.

### 3. See the decision

1. Tap the **Completed** tab. Reviewed work is marked **Approved** or **Rejected**.
2. Tap an item to open it.
3. Read **Review feedback**. It is what your steward wrote to you.

The **Completed** tab starts on the last month. If you are looking for something older, change the time filter to **Year**.

If you also review other people's work, choose **My work** in the Completed filter to see the decisions on your own submissions.

## How you know it worked

- **Approved:** the item is on the **Completed** tab, marked **Approved**. Its page says **Your submission has been approved**.
- **Rejected:** the item is marked **Rejected**, and its page says **Not yet approved**, with the steward's reason under **Review feedback**.

## What happens next

### While it is in review

A steward of your garden looks at your photos and details and compares them with what the action asks for. Home's **Notifications** bell can list your work's approval or rejection, with review feedback. It shows events for the selected garden and marks them seen on this device when you open it.

This is an in-app list, not a push notification or email. Offline or incomplete reads may not show every event. **Your Work** and the work's own record remain the places to check its status.

There is no fixed review time. It depends on your stewards. Two things help:

- **Tell your steward** when you have submitted something, in the chat your garden already uses.
- **Do not wait until an action is about to close.** A steward can only decide while the action is still open. If your work is still **In review** as the end date gets close, remind them.

### If it is approved

An approval is your steward's signed decision that the work happened as you described it. It is a separate record, linked to yours, with their feedback and how confident they are.

What approval brings:

- **A public record.** Approved work appears on your garden's page on the public website, with its photos, your description, and your account's name if it has one.
- **Proof that travels.** The work and the approval stay readable outside Green Goods. On the work's page, under **Actions**, tap **View Certificate** to open the public record.
- **A place in your garden's story.** Approved work is what a garden can later bundle into an impact certificate and show to funders.

What approval does not do:

- **It does not pay you by itself.** No payment is attached to an approval.
- **It does not measure the outcome.** It records a steward's review of your evidence. It is not a scientific measurement of what changed.

### Work for a promise

If your work contributes to a promise, open that promise from the garden's **Promises** tab. **Linked** means the work is attached to it. **Approved** means a steward reviewed the work. **Counted toward this promise** means that approval reached its requirement. If approved work is still waiting to be counted, ask your steward to check the existing record; do not upload a duplicate.

Once the required work is counted, the eligible confirmer acts next. Confirmation that the promise was kept is separate from work approval and from payment. The [promise guide](/community/gardener-guide/make-a-promise) covers both garden-work and other-proof paths.

### How gardeners get rewarded

Rewards are separate from approval, and each garden decides how to use them.

| Reward path | What it means |
|---|---|
| A cookie-jar claim | A separate claim from a garden's shared fund, subject to access, balance, limits and cooldown |
| An agreed voucher reward | Access to the issuer's stated service under an eligible pilot promise's exact terms |
| An agreed G$ reward | A separate monetary reward, only when included in the promise's accepted terms |

Submitting ordinary work does not create any of these rewards automatically.

**The garden's shared fund.** If your garden has a cookie jar with money in it, eligible gardeners can claim from it. Ask your steward what the jar is meant for before you claim. A jar claim is separate from any reward agreed for your work.

1. Open **Home**, tap the wallet button at the top to open **Your Wallet**, and choose **Cookies**.
2. Find the right garden heading and tap its jar row to open the claim form. Your wallet can list jars from more than one garden.
3. Read the jar's limit per claim and how often you can claim.
4. Enter an amount and say what it is for under **Purpose**. The purpose is recorded with the payout.
5. Tap **Claim**, review the confirmation, and confirm when asked.

If no jars appear, the app has not found one you can currently access. An offline or failed read is not proof that the garden has no jar. Read any warning, reconnect and retry, or ask your steward.

**Badges.** Open **Profile**, then **Badges**. **First Work** can be claimed after your first submitted work.

### Pilot voucher rewards

The voucher pilot is limited to participating members and supported promises. Read the issuer's terms before taking part. A voucher names access to a particular service; it is not a promise of cash or an automatic booking.

1. From Home, open **Promises** and choose the request. Read its **Rewards**: who issues the voucher, how many you are agreeing to, the work required and any separate G$ reward.
2. If offered, open **Agree to the Rewards** and review the exact terms. **Agree and Take It Up** records your agreement before take-up. These can be separate wallet or passkey prompts. If steward approval is required, wait for acceptance; agreeing does not reserve voucher inventory or a service appointment.
3. Record the agreed work. Follow it through **Linked**, **Approved**, and **Counted toward this promise**. For a promise using other proof, follow its evidence steps instead.
4. The eligible confirmer acts when the promise is ready. **Kept** records that confirmation; it does not prove a reward transfer arrived.
5. Check each reward separately. Vouchers and G$ can arrive independently. When voucher holdings are available, **Your Wallet** has a **Vouchers** tab. A missing tab, row or receipt does not prove that a transfer failed; ask your steward before retrying anything.

:::note Current beta limit
The beta can show agreed voucher terms before confirmation, but it does not yet show live reward-delivery receipts after a promise is kept. A missing reward row does not prove that a reward arrived or that a transfer failed. Ask your steward to check the agreed reward and its delivery record. Do not submit the work again.
:::

Receiving a voucher is separate from using its service. A suitable held voucher can be presented for that service when its booking path is available. Exchanging for another issuer's voucher is optional and does not book the service. Read the exact cost, availability, consent and issuer-approval steps before continuing; do not assume a listed offer means immediate access.

Where the service supports a partial return, the holder approves the final delivered and refundable amounts. A proposed return is not money or vouchers already back in your account. Each transfer must finish separately. Ask the issuer for help if the service or a return remains unresolved.

### If it is rejected

A rejection is a decision about that one record. It is not a judgment of you, and it is the most useful feedback you will get.

1. Read **Review feedback** on the work's page.
2. Fix exactly what it names: a clearer photo, a missing number, a different action.
3. [Submit the work again](/community/gardener-guide/uploading-your-work) as a new submission. The rejected one stays in **Completed** and cannot be edited.

If the feedback is unclear, ask your steward before you resubmit.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Your work is not in **Pending** or **Completed** | Check the account and the garden selected on Home. Then set the **Completed** time filter to **Year** |
| It has been **In review** for a long time | Message your steward. If the action has ended, they can no longer decide on it, and you will need to submit under an action that is open |
| The label is **Checking** and will not change | Leave it. The app is making sure it arrived, and it checks again when you are online. Do not submit it again |
| **Approved**, but you expected a payment | Approval does not pay by itself. See [How gardeners get rewarded](#how-gardeners-get-rewarded) |
| A kept pilot promise has no reward receipt | The beta's live delivery view is incomplete. Ask your steward to check the agreed reward; do not submit duplicate work |
| The list looks out of date | Tap the refresh button next to **Upload all**. It needs a signal |
