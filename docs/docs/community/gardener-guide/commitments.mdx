# Make a Promise

Make an offer when you can provide help, or a request when you need it. A promise records the agreement and who will confirm it was kept.

  <img src="/img/community/guide-make.webp" alt="A seedling-care station with a watering can, empty gloves and a closed notebook." width="1672" height="941" loading="eager" />
</figure>


## Before you start

- Sign in with the account that joined this garden. Visitors can read records; taking part requires membership.
- Check that the garden has an open pool. New money rewards and promise payouts are deferred.
- Photos and details can become public before you send the work record. Use your own evidence and read the [privacy guidance](/faq#is-what-i-upload-private).
- Agree the amount, deadline, team and evidence with the people involved.

## Steps

### Read the agreement

1. Open your garden in the app and tap **Promises**.
2. Open a promise and read its terms, work requirements, team, and confirmation details. A season is the garden's main period of work; a campaign is a focused effort beside it. A promise can also stand on its own.
3. Check which part is yours:

| Kind | Who provides the help? | Who normally confirms it? |
|---|---|---|
| **Offer** | The person making the offer | The person it is made to or who takes it up |
| **Request** | The person who takes up the request | The person who asked |

A promise can name a group of confirmers instead. The person doing the work and everyone on its contributing team are excluded from confirming it, including through a steward fallback.

**Example:** you offer a seedling-care session, and a neighbor takes it up. You do the session and supply evidence; your neighbor confirms it. If your neighbor requests the session first and you take it up, you still provide the help and your neighbor still confirms it.

### Make your offer or request

1. Use **+** in the garden's Promises and choose an offer or request.
2. Follow the questions about the kind of help, how much, and its details. Garden work names the garden's actions; other help uses proof such as photos, notes, or links.
3. Read the final review, including who acts next, then choose **Make This Offer**, **Make This Request**, or **Request This Work**, as shown.

### Withdraw before it is taken up

Before anyone takes it up, the creator may see **Withdraw This**. It needs a connection and a reason; review **Withdraw This Offer** or **Withdraw This Request** before confirming. Once someone has taken it up, ask your steward about the existing record. Withdrawal is not a universal undo, and taking it up is different from joining a team, which also needs a connection.

## How you know it worked

The promise appears in **Promises** with the intended terms and a status showing it reached the shared record. A waiting or sending item has not finished.

## What happens next

Someone takes the promise up. If steward approval is required, that decision comes first. Share [Keep a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/keep-a-promise) with the person providing the help.

## If something goes wrong

A saved item on this phone is not yet a shared record. Keep the same account and device, check the status and history before retrying, and avoid duplicates. [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync) explains the send states.

If **Promises are not ready here yet** appears, ask your steward whether the pool is open. Withdrawal needs a connection and a reason; after take-up, ask your steward about the existing record.

Ask your steward about the agreement or record. For an app problem, open **Profile → Help**.
