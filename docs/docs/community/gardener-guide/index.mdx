# Gardener Guide

A gardener is anyone doing the work a garden exists for: planting trees, clearing waste, keeping solar panels running, teaching a class. Green Goods gives that work a record, so it does not disappear the moment it is done.

What you get from documenting your work:

- **Proof you can point to.** Each piece of work becomes a record with your photos and details, reviewed by a steward from your own garden.
- **It works where you work.** You can record with no signal and send when you are back in range.
- **Your garden gets seen.** Approved work is what your garden shows to the people who fund it.

## The path

Do these in order the first time. After that, most days are step 3.

1. [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating). Put the app on your phone and create your account.
2. [Join a Garden](/community/gardener-guide/joining-a-garden). Tell the app which community your work belongs to.
3. [Submit Work](/community/gardener-guide/uploading-your-work). Choose what you did, add photos and details, and send it.
4. [Track Your Work](/community/gardener-guide/track-status-and-attestations). See where each submission stands, what approval brings, and what to do if it comes back.
5. [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync). Send what is still waiting on your phone and fix what will not go.

## Promises {#promises}

Promises are optional. You can submit and recover ordinary work without one.

- [Make a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/make-a-promise): offer help or request it.
- [Keep a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/keep-a-promise): take it up, join a team, and record evidence.
- [Confirm a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/confirm-a-promise): independently check whether it was kept.

## Three things to know first

- **Use one account.** Your gardens, drafts, and history belong to the account you created. Signing in a different way makes a different account.
- **Your work is saved on your phone first.** Nothing is lost if the signal drops. It waits in **Your Work** until you send it.
- **What you submit is public and permanent.** Photos and details become part of your garden's public record and cannot be deleted. Only add what you are comfortable sharing. The [FAQ](/faq#is-what-i-upload-private) has the details.

## Getting help

In the app, open **Profile** and choose the **Help** tab. It links back to this guide and to the Green Goods Telegram group. Your steward is the best person to ask about your garden's actions and what counts as good evidence.
