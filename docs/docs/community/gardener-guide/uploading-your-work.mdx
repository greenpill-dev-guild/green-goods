# Submit Work

Submit a clear record of something you did in your garden so a steward can review it. You pick what you did and where, add photos, fill in a few details, and send it.

## Before you start

- **Sign in with the account that joined the garden.**
- **Be a member of the garden.** Only gardens you belong to appear when you submit. See [Join a Garden](/community/gardener-guide/joining-a-garden).
- **Know which action matches your work.** An action is the kind of work your garden has agreed to track, such as planting, a clean-up, or a panel check. If two seem close, ask your steward before you submit.
- **Have your evidence ready:** photos, and a short video or voice note if it helps.
- **Weak signal is fine.** Your work is saved on your phone as you go, and you can send it later.


## Steps

These navigation steps follow the current beta. Use the version your steward asked you to use; [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating) explains choosing the beta.

The top of the screen shows where you are: **Start**, **Media**, **Details**, **Review**.

### Step 1: Choose what you did

Your work goes to the garden selected on **Home**. Check its name first. To work in another garden, tap the name and choose that garden in **Your Gardens** before opening the submission flow.

1. Tap **Garden** in the bar at the bottom of the app.
2. If this work is for a promise you already took up, choose it under **Keep a Promise**. For an available request you have not taken up yet, choose it under **Take Up an Ask**. Read the terms and any approval or signing steps before continuing. For a voucher-reward request, first open its promise page from **Promises** on Home and follow the [pilot reward agreement](/community/gardener-guide/track-status-and-attestations#pilot-voucher-rewards) there.
3. A promise with actions opens its own choices. Tap **Add Evidence** if it takes other proof, or choose the action that matches your contribution. An evidence-only promise opens its evidence steps directly.
4. For work without a promise, choose **Log Work on Its Own**. If no promises or asks are available, the action choices appear directly under **Select Your Action**. Use the domain chips to narrow them, then tap your action.

Choosing a promise or action moves you forward. With one action and no promises or asks to choose from, the app can open **Media** directly. A draft or a link may already name a different garden. Check **Logging work in** before continuing. If no garden is available for this flow, answer **Select Your Garden** first.

Work does not need a promise. The [promise guide](/community/gardener-guide/keep-a-promise#record-your-contribution) explains linking existing work and the difference between linked, approved, and counted. Taking up a request can involve more than one wallet or passkey prompt; wait for the current step rather than starting it again.

If you left work unfinished earlier, the app asks **Continue Previous Work?** first. Tap **Continue Draft** to pick it up, or **Start Fresh** to begin a new one. The draft stays in **Your Work** either way.

> Check the garden and choose the right action before you add evidence. A correct submission is much easier to approve.

### Step 2: Add evidence

Evidence should make the work easy to verify.

:::warning Choose what you make public
Uploaded photos and other media are public. When you are online, the app can prepare queued photos and details in public storage before you tap **Upload all**. Preparing those files is separate from sending the work record. Only include what you are comfortable making public; see [what to keep out of your photos](/faq#what-should-i-keep-out-of-my-photos).
:::

1. Read the line under **Upload Media**. It says what this action needs to see.
2. Use **Choose from Your Photos** or **Take a Photo**. Turn on **Record a voice note** if a spoken explanation would help.
3. Watch the counter, for example **1 of 2 photos**. It turns into **2 photos added** when you have enough.
4. Retake anything blurry, dark, or incomplete before you move on.
5. Tap **Details**. It stays gray until the action has the photos it needs.

Limits: up to 10 files and 50 MB in one submission. Each photo can be up to 10 MB. A video can be up to 20 MB and 30 seconds long. A video does not count toward the photos an action needs.

> A clear photo usually beats a long explanation.

Use your own photos, taken where and when the work happened. That is what makes the record worth trusting.

### Step 3: Add details

Details are different for each action. Your steward's team chose the questions.

1. Fill in each field. A red star marks a field you must answer.
2. Use real numbers. Do not type placeholder text where a count or a measurement belongs.
3. **Time Spent (hours)** is optional. Decimals are fine, so 1.5 means an hour and a half.
4. **Share Location** is optional and off until you turn it on. It adds your approximate location, to about 100 meters, and that location is published with the work.
5. Add a note under **Feedback** if it helps the steward understand what happened.
6. Tap **Review Work**. It stays gray until every required field is filled.

> Make the details concrete enough for a steward to verify without guessing.

### Step 4: Review and upload

One last look prevents most rework.

1. Check the garden, the photos, and your answers.
2. If anything is wrong, use the back arrow and fix it.
3. Tap **Upload Work**.
4. Confirm when your phone or wallet asks. With a passkey, use your fingerprint, face, or screen lock.

### If you are offline

Green Goods is built for the field.

- The steps are the same without a signal, as long as you have opened the app online before. When you tap **Upload Work**, a message says **Saved offline**, and the work waits on your phone.
- When you are back online, open **Your Work** and tap **Upload all**. Nothing is added to the garden's record until you do.
- Do not submit the same work twice because the first one has not gone yet. Keep the queued copy; clearing app storage or removing the app can lose work you have not sent.

## How you know it worked

- **Online:** a message says **Work uploaded**. The app takes you to **Your Work**, where the item is marked **In review**.
- **Offline:** a message says **Saved offline**. In **Your Work** the item is marked **To upload**.

## What happens next

A steward of your garden reviews the work. They either approve it or reject it with a note on what to fix. Check **Your Work** or Home's **Notifications** bell for the selected garden. The bell lists in-app events; it does not send a push notification or email. [Track Your Work](/community/gardener-guide/track-status-and-attestations) explains the statuses and feedback.

Once work is sent, it becomes part of the garden's public record and cannot be edited or deleted. If you made a mistake, tell your steward. They can reject it so you can submit a corrected one.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| No actions to choose from | The garden has no action open right now. Ask your steward |
| Your garden is not in the list | You are not a member yet, or you are signed in to a different account. See [Join a Garden](/community/gardener-guide/joining-a-garden) |
| **Details** or **Review Work** stays gray | A photo the action needs or a field with a red star is missing |
| **Some files were not added** | The file is not a photo or video the app can read, or it is over the size limits above |
| **Upload canceled** | You closed the signing confirmation. The work record was not sent. Find it in **Your Work**, on **Pending**, ready to upload or discard. Media may already have been prepared in public storage |
| **Connection unstable** | The work is saved and waiting. Send it from **Your Work** when your signal is steady |
| A draft says a photo is no longer readable | Remove that photo, then add it again from the **Media** step |
| **You have 20 drafts** | Delete a draft you no longer need to make room. Your other drafts are safe |

For anything that will not send, see [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync). To reach a person, ask in [Discord](https://discord.gg/greenpill).
