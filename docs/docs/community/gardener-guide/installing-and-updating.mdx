# Install and Update the App

Green Goods is an app you install from the website, not from an app store. Installing puts it on your home screen, and it is what lets you record work when you have no signal. You only need to do this once per phone.

## Before you start

- **An Android phone with Chrome, or an iPhone with Safari.** Other browsers cannot install the app. Green Goods tells you if you need to switch.
- **A connection.** Installing and signing in both need one. Recording work afterwards does not.
- **A few minutes.** The whole app is a few megabytes.

## Install on Android

1. Open [greengoods.app](https://greengoods.app) in Chrome.
2. Tap **Install App**. On a phone it is the first button on the home page. You can also find it at the bottom of the menu.
3. Confirm when Chrome asks to install.
4. Open Green Goods from your home screen.

If Chrome does not ask, Green Goods shows **Install Green Goods on this phone** with two steps instead: tap Chrome's menu (the three dots), then choose **Add to Home Screen** or **Install App**.

## Install on iPhone

1. Open [greengoods.app](https://greengoods.app) in Safari.
2. Tap **Install App**.
3. Follow the two steps Green Goods shows: tap the Share button in Safari's bar, then scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Open Green Goods from your home screen.

## Create your account

Always open Green Goods from the home screen. A browser tab shows the public website, not the app.

1. Tap **Create Account**.
2. Type a name of at least three characters. Write it down somewhere safe. You will need it to sign in on another phone.
3. Tap **Create Account** again, then confirm with your fingerprint, face, or screen lock when your phone asks.

That is your account. There is no password to remember, because your phone keeps a passkey for you.

**Already have an account?** Tap **Already have an account?**, enter your name, and tap **Sign in with Passkey**. Your passkey usually travels with your Apple or Google account, so the new phone needs to be signed in to that same account.

**Using your own wallet?** Tap **Sign in with a Wallet** instead. A wallet and a passkey are two different accounts, so pick one and keep using it.

## How you know it worked

- **Android:** a message says **App installed**, then **Getting it ready to work offline**, and finally **Ready to work offline**. On a fast connection the middle step can pass too quickly to see. On an iPhone there is usually no message. The icon on your home screen is the sign.
- **After signing in:** you land on **Home**. The first time, a message says **You're signed in** and offers **Open Help**.

## What happens next

Nothing is waiting on anyone else. Your next step is to join the garden you work with.

In the background, the app saves what it needs to work without a signal. You can watch it under **Profile**, on the **Account** tab, in the **Offline** row. It keeps your gardens' work lists, plus photos for the garden you have open and for your own work. **Pause** stops it and **Resume** continues. If your phone has Data Saver on, photos wait until you tap **Resume** or turn Data Saver off.

## Update the app

Green Goods checks for a new version when you open it and when you come back to it. **An update never applies on its own.** It waits for you, so it cannot interrupt work you are in the middle of.

1. Open **Profile** and stay on the **Account** tab.
2. Under **Settings**, find the **Update** row.
3. Tap **Check**. If a new version is ready, tap **Restart**.
4. The app reloads and says **Updated**.

If you leave the app open for a long time, a message may offer **Restart to Update**. It does the same thing.

| The Update row says | What is happening | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Check for a newer version. | Nothing yet | Tap **Check** |
| Looking for a newer version. | Checking now | Wait |
| Installing the latest version. | Downloading in the background | Wait. You can keep working |
| A new version is ready. | Waiting for you | Tap **Restart** |
| Finish saving or sending your work before restarting. | A draft is saving, or work is being sent | Let it finish, then tap **Restart** |
| Restarting the app. | Applying now | Wait |
| Close every app window, then open it again. | The restart did not finish | Close the app fully and open it again |
| Couldn't finish. Try again. | The download failed | Tap **Try Again** |

Two tips for updating:

- **Update while you have a connection, not in the field.** A restart with no signal still works, but the app cannot fetch its newer files until you are back online.
- **Send what is waiting first.** Open **Your Work** and tap **Upload all**. The update will not lose it. Uploading needs a connection, and the field often does not have one.

:::warning Never clear site data or app storage
Clearing the browser's site data or storage for Green Goods **deletes work you have not sent**: drafts, and anything waiting in **Your Work**. Removing the app from your phone can do the same. There is no button for this in the app, because it is never a safe fix. If you are stuck, use the list below.
:::

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A message says your browser cannot install apps | On Android, tap **Open in Chrome**. On iPhone, tap **Copy Link** and open the link in Safari. This also happens when you open the link inside another app, such as a chat app |
| On Android, a sheet says **Install in Chrome** | You are in Brave, which saves a shortcut, not the full app. Tap **Open in Chrome** and install from there |
| You only see the public website, with no sign-in | You opened a browser tab. Open Green Goods from the home screen icon |
| **A new version is ready**, but **Restart** does nothing | Something is still saving or sending. Wait for it to finish, then tap **Restart** again. Work that is only waiting to upload does not block the update |
| The app restarted but looks the same | Close it fully and open it again. The **Updated** message is easy to miss |
| It keeps asking you to restart | Close every window of the app, including browser tabs on the same site, then open it again |
| A photo from your iPhone will not attach | The file that reads iPhone photos has not arrived yet, usually because you installed or restarted with no signal, or Data Saver is on. Turn Data Saver off, open the app on a good connection, and give it a minute. Your work still saves |
| You cannot sign in | Signing in needs a connection. Recording work does not |
| You cannot tell which version you have | The app does not show a version number. Tap **Check** in the **Update** row. **No update available** means you are current |

"Close the app fully" means removing it from your recent apps, not only going back to the home screen.

Still stuck? Open **Profile**, then the **Help** tab, and tap **Telegram** to ask the community.
