# Manage Actions

An action is a kind of work your garden records, such as planting a tree, sorting a load of waste, or checking a solar panel. Each action says what to photograph and which details to give. When a gardener submits work, they choose an action first.

  <img src="/img/screenshots/guide-garden-actions.webp" alt="Edit Garden domain controls show Solar and Agroforestry enabled and Education and Waste disabled." loading="eager" width="656" height="249" />

Actions are shared across all gardens, and the Green Goods team maintains the list. As a steward, your job is to make sure your garden shows the right ones, and to speak up when one is missing or wrong.

## How an action reaches your gardeners

A gardener sees an action when both of these are true:

- **Your garden covers that area of work.** Every action belongs to one of four areas: Solar, Agroforestry, Education, or Waste. Your garden shows the actions for the areas it has turned on.
- **The action is open.** Every action has a start date and an end date. Before the start it is upcoming. After the end it is closed.

The end date matters twice. Gardeners cannot submit to a closed action, and **you cannot approve or reject work once its action has closed.** Review before the end date. The public list does not show an action's dates, so ask the Green Goods team when you need to know when one closes.

## Before you start

- Be a steward or owner of the garden, and check that its name shows at the top left of the dashboard.
- Know which kinds of work your gardeners really do.

## Steps

### 1. See which actions exist

1. Open [greengoods.app/actions](https://greengoods.app/actions). It lists the published actions.
2. Use the filter to look at one area of work at a time.
3. Note the actions your garden needs, and any that are missing.

To see exactly what your gardeners see, open the Green Goods app, tap **Garden**, and look under **Select Your Action** with your garden selected.

### 2. Change your garden's areas of work

1. In the dashboard, open the **Garden** workspace and tap **Edit Garden**.
2. Under **Domains**, turn areas on or off. At least one stays on.
3. Tap **Save Changes** and confirm.

### 3. Ask for a new action or a fix

You cannot add or edit an action yourself unless you maintain the list. Ask the Green Goods team in the [community on Discord](https://discord.gg/greenpill). To make the request easy to act on, send:

- **What the work is**, in one sentence.
- **Which area** it belongs to.
- **What a good photo shows**, and how many photos you want.
- **The details to ask for**, such as a count, a weight, or a measurement.
- **The dates** it should be open.

Ask early. A new action has to exist before your gardeners do the work, and it has to stay open long enough for you to review it.

## How you know it worked

- **Areas of work:** after you save, your gardeners see the actions for the areas you turned on, the next time they open the **Garden** tab in the app.
- **A new action:** it appears on [greengoods.app/actions](https://greengoods.app/actions), and in the app once its start date arrives.

## What happens next

Tell your gardeners which action to use for which work. Most rejected submissions come from the wrong action, not from bad work. A short message in your garden's chat ("for the Saturday clean-up, use this action") saves everyone a round trip.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A gardener sees **No active actions at this time** | Nothing is open in your garden's areas of work right now. Ask the Green Goods team which actions are open, and to extend or add one |
| A gardener cannot find an action you can see on the website | Its area of work is turned off for your garden, or the action is not open right now. Check **Edit Garden** first, then ask the Green Goods team about its dates |
| **Action expired** when you try to review work | The action closed before you decided, and the decision can no longer be recorded. Ask the gardener to submit again under an action that is open |
| Gardeners keep choosing the wrong action | The instructions are probably unclear. Tell your gardeners which one to use, and ask the Green Goods team to tighten the wording |
| No **Actions** workspace in the dashboard | That workspace is only for people who maintain the list. Everything on this page works without it |

## If you maintain the action list

This part is for accounts with deployer access. You have an **Actions** workspace in the dashboard.

1. Open **Actions** and tap **Create Action**.
2. **Basics:** enter the **Title**, the **Slug**, the **Domain**, the **Start date**, and the **End date**.
3. **Capitals & Media:** choose the **Forms of capital** the work builds, and add example images if you have them.
4. **Instructions:** set the questions gardeners answer, how many photos the action needs, and what the review screen shows. You can start from a template.
5. **Review:** read it through, tap **Create Action**, and confirm once.

A finished action reads **Action registered**. To change one later, open it from the **Actions** list and tap **Edit**.


Write instructions the way a gardener would read them on a phone, in the field: one short title, exactly what the photo should show, and only the questions a reviewer needs answered.
