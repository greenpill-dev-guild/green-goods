# Run a Commitment Pool

<StewardPathNav />

A pool is your garden's shared place for promises and the rules for taking part. Use it to agree what help is needed, follow the evidence, and confirm what was kept.

:::info Scope of the 2.0 release
Garden promises, work linking, participation review, and confirmation are included. New money-reward promises, settlement-account configuration, and payout actions are deferred, even where an older funding record is readable. Use promises without a money reward in this guide; work approval and confirmation do not pay anyone.
:::

## Before you start

- Sign in to [the steward dashboard](https://admin.greengoods.app) with access to this garden. [Get steward access](/community/steward-guide/#get-steward-access) if needed.
- Check the garden name at the top left, then open **Garden → Promises**. Each review names the pool it affects; read that target before signing.
- The pool must be registered. If the page says it is not registered or cannot be read, resolve that with the person responsible for your garden's setup. A read failure does not prove there is no pool.
- Agree the purpose, per-person commitment limit, season or campaign, evidence rules, and independent confirmers with your garden. Have a steady connection for setup and direct changes.

## Steps

### 1. Set up the pool and open a season

If the pool is **Not Ready**, choose **Set Up Commitments**. If it is already taking commitments, read its existing rules and continue to step 2.

:::warning Opening fixes the split
A season or campaign's split becomes fixed when it opens. Read it before signing. A prepared season is only written down; people cannot make promises in it until it opens.
:::

1. In **How It Works**, write what the pool is for and how many open commitments one person may hold.
2. In **The Season**, enter its name and dates. A season is the main period of work, with only one running at a time. A campaign is a focused effort that can run beside it, or on its own.
3. In **The Split**, review how recognition is shared. Make each allocation add up to 100%. This records terms; it does not distribute money.
4. In **Open**, check the target, the changes, and the wallet-prompt count. Choose **Set Up and Open**. For a later season or campaign, use the opening action shown for that case.
5. Keep the flow open and follow its progress until the season is shown as open. Several changes may share one wallet prompt, or need separate prompts.

Use **Pool Settings** to change the agreement or commitment limit later. A save can contain more than one change. If it stops partway, the successful changes remain recorded; inspect the progress and current pool before retrying what remains.

### 2. Seed promises people can take up

Choose **Seed Promises**. As an illustrative example, your garden can request seedling-care sessions with a required planting action and a named independent reviewer.

1. In **What**, choose whether the pool offers help or requests it, the kind of promise, its title, and its season or campaign.
2. In **How Much**, set how many promises to create and what each asks for. For garden work, choose the required action rows and counts. Check the team policy and deadline.
3. In **Proof & Confirmation**, choose who can confirm, how many confirmations are needed, and whether taking it up needs steward approval. The provider and contributing team cannot confirm their own promise. A named group must have enough independent people to meet its threshold.
4. Leave **Does this come with a reward?** at **No** for this release. A displayed old reward or active contract is not permission to create a new money reward.
5. In **Review**, check each answer. Use **Add Another** if preparing different promises together, then choose **Create Promise** or **Create … Promises**, as shown.
6. Read the result for every promise. A created or queued item is not the same as one recorded by the shared system. Return to **Promises** and use **Send Now** only for the row whose status asks for it.

**Success:** the intended promises appear in the pool with the right terms and current status. A partly completed batch stays partly completed; do not recreate its successful rows.

### 3. Review who takes a promise up

Open **Review Promises** in the pool. For each waiting request, check the promise, the account or garden taking it up, and any reason before choosing **Accept** or declining it.

Acceptance sends directly when you choose **Accept** and confirm any signing prompt. Check the result before acting again. A request from a steward on behalf of another garden names that garden as the provider; the person who sent the request is a separate identity.

The pool may also allow eligible members to take promises up directly. Those do not need a separate participation decision from you.

### 4. Keep work review and promise confirmation separate

**Before readiness, check the team and contributions.** Readiness freezes them. Nobody on that team may confirm the promise, even if they also have a steward role.

For **garden work**, use the existing [Review Work guide](/community/steward-guide/reviewing-work) to review the linked submission. A work item can be linked and approved without its approval being counted toward the promise yet. If it says **Approved · waiting for a steward to count it**:

1. In **Garden → Promises**, open the affected promise and inspect its requirements and **Recovery** section.
2. When **Count Approved Linked Work** shows approved links waiting, choose **Count Linked Work**.
3. Read the named promise and the warning that counting can fix the team, then confirm **Count Linked Work**.
4. Wait for **Approved linked work counted.** If it reads **Waiting for indexed confirmation…**, check that result before retrying. If a newer work decision needs review, return to the work record first. Do not ask the gardener to upload a duplicate.

If the promise requires an assessment, ask an evaluator or steward to [make an assessment](/community/evaluator-guide/making-an-assessment) for the provider garden. Before readiness, choose **Attach Assessment…** when offered, then review **Attach an Assessment** and choose the current assessment that applies. If none is listed, record it for that provider garden first; an assessment from another garden does not fill the requirement.

Counted work that meets all required rows, together with any required assessment, can make the promise ready for confirmation automatically. Do not use **Send for Confirmation** as the garden-work readiness step. Other supported proof flows have their own send-for-confirmation action.

For confirmation:

1. Open **Hub → Confirm** and check the selected garden in the header. It scopes your authority; a row can concern another garden's pool if this garden has permission to confirm it.
2. Open **Confirm Kept…** and read the target, provider, evidence, and confirmation count.
3. Confirm only if the promise was kept and you are eligible. Your confirmation may be one of several required; watch the count after it arrives.

Fallback and disputed decisions have separate reviews and record their reason. Do not use fallback to approve your own contribution. Protocol-wide coordination lives in **Community → Coordination** and is outside this garden runbook.

### 5. End a season, then decide whether to archive it

1. Check that every commitment in the season has finished. If the page says some are still live, follow those records first. The end date alone does not finish them.
2. Choose **End Season…** or **End Campaign…**, check the named target, and confirm while online. The record becomes **Reconciled** and stops accepting commitments in that period.
3. If the garden wants an impact certificate and certificate creation is available, [make it while the season is reconciled](/community/steward-guide/creating-impact-certificates).
4. Only then consider **Archive…**. Read **Archive This Season** or **Archive This Campaign** before confirming. Archiving is final and prevents making a certificate from that cycle later; its history remains readable.

## How you know it worked

- The pool and its intended season show they are open, rather than merely prepared.
- Each seeded promise has its own visible result; nothing remains only in the local queue unnoticed.
- Kept promises show the required confirmation result and who confirmed them.
- An ended season reads **Reconciled**. It is separate from the later archive action.

## What happens next

Members make or take up promises, the people providing help add evidence, stewards review required work, and eligible confirmers decide whether the promise was kept. Share the [gardener guide](/community/gardener-guide/commitments) so each person knows their part.

A kept promise is a coordination result. This release does not send new promise payouts. Existing transfer histories may still show pending or completed records; dispatch is not proof that money arrived. The [funding guide](/community/steward-guide/funding-and-governance) explains the separate shared fund and endowment.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| Setup stopped after some changes | Read the progress list and existing pool or season. Keep what landed; retry only the unfinished changes |
| A wallet prompt closed without a clear result | Check the current record and transaction status first. An uncertain broadcast needs checking, not an assumed retry |
| A promise remains on this device | Use its current send/retry control. Keep successful rows and avoid creating the same promise again |
| The pool is paused | Read why. An authorized steward can use **Resume Pool** when appropriate; wait for the updated status before continuing |
| A second season cannot open | Finish the running season, or start a campaign beside it. Do not create duplicate seasons to bypass the rule |
| Work is approved but the promise is waiting | Check the exact linked requirement, counting status, other required work, and any required assessment |
| A required assessment is missing | Record it for the provider garden, then use **Attach Assessment…** before readiness; see [Make an Assessment](/community/evaluator-guide/making-an-assessment) |
| You cannot confirm | Check whose confirmation is required, the threshold, the selected garden, and whether you contributed. Steward access does not override self-confirmation rules |
| The funding record is visible but money controls are missing | New money rewards and settlement actions are deferred in this release. Do not retry them through another screen |

Ask another steward or the garden owner about the record first. For an app problem, open **Settings → Support** in the dashboard. On a phone, find **Settings** under **Profile**. Share the affected promise's link.
