# Create a Garden

A garden is the home for one community's work: its people, its records, and its funds. This page is for the person who sets a new one up.

<GuideImage src="/img/screenshots/admin-create-garden-details.png" alt="The Create Garden form in the Green Goods Hub, with its Garden Details section showing fields for the garden name, location, web name and description, and an optional banner image" loading="eager" width="1616" height="1224" className="gg-figure" />

**Not everyone can create a garden.** Your account has to be on the list of garden creators, which the Green Goods team keeps. If you want a garden for your community and are not on the list, ask in the [Green Goods community on Discord](https://discord.gg/greenpill). If your garden already exists, skip to [Manage Members](/community/steward-guide/managing-members).

## Before you start

Have these ready so you can finish in one sitting:

- **The account you sign in with** on the list of garden creators.
- **A name** for the garden, and **where it is**.
- **A short description** of the community and its work.
- **The areas of work** the garden covers: Solar, Agroforestry, Education, or Waste. Pick only the ones it really does. They decide which actions its gardeners can record.
- **A banner image**, if you have one. It is optional.
- **Your first stewards and gardeners**, as account addresses or names ending in `.eth`. This is optional too. You can add people later.
- **A connection.** The form saves your progress as you type, and creating the garden needs you online.

## Steps

### 1. Open the form

1. Sign in to the Green Goods Hub at [admin.greengoods.app](https://admin.greengoods.app).
2. Tap the search button at the top, or press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac), and choose **Create Garden**.

If you do not have a garden yet, the **No garden access yet** screen has its own **Create Garden** button.

The form is one page with three numbered sections.

### 2. Fill in garden details

1. Enter the **Garden name**.
2. Enter the **Location**.
3. Check the **Web name**. The form suggests one from the name. It is the garden's short name on the web, ending in `.greengoods.eth`, so keep it simple.
4. Write the **Description**.
5. Add a **Banner image** if you have one.
6. Under **Action domains**, choose the areas of work.

### 3. Add your first team

1. Under **Stewards**, paste an address or name and tap **Add**. Repeat for each steward.
2. Do the same under **Gardeners**.

Stewards can already do everything a gardener can, so you do not need to add them to both lists. You can leave both lists empty. An address you typed and did not add stops the garden from being created until you add it or clear the field.

A new garden is **Invite only** unless you turn on **Open joining**, which lets anyone join as a gardener. You can change this later.

<GuideImage src="/img/screenshots/guide-garden-team.webp" alt="The team step of Create Garden: a First team note saying these people are added when the garden is created, Open joining switched off so the garden is invite only, and one steward and two gardeners added by address." loading="lazy" width="1376" height="1272" className="gg-figure" maxWidth="672px">

You can check these people’s roles under Members after creation.

</GuideImage>

### 4. Review and create

1. Read **Review and Create**. It shows everything you entered and what creating the garden costs.
2. Fix anything that looks wrong.
3. Tap **Create Garden** and approve the request.
4. Keep the page open until it finishes.

<GuideImage src="/img/screenshots/guide-garden-review.webp" alt="Riverside Commons Garden's review shows Invite only, two planned gardeners and one steward, a network fee of about 0.000477 ETH, and a name fee covered by Green Goods." loading="lazy" width="1568" height="1150" className="gg-figure" maxWidth="672px">

Check the joining setting and your first team before creating the garden.

</GuideImage>

## How you know it worked

- A message says **Garden created**.
- The Hub takes you to your new garden, which is already selected. Its records can take a moment to fill in.
- If you added people, a second message asks you to **Check your first team**.

You are the garden's first owner.

## What happens next

You act next. The garden exists, and it is empty.

1. **Check the team.** Open **Community** and confirm the people you added are listed with the right roles. See [Manage Members](/community/steward-guide/managing-members).
2. **Decide who can join.** The same page shows how to change open joining.
3. **Check its setup.** Creating a garden sets up its web name, community, pools, vaults, and cookie jars on its own. If a part is missing or still on its way, a **Garden Setup** card appears on the garden's **Health** tab. It finishes what you can finish from there, and says when the Green Goods team needs to finish the rest.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Unauthorized** when you open the form | Your account is not on the list of garden creators. Ask the Green Goods team |
| A field is marked as required | Fill it in. The garden name, location, web name, description, and at least one domain are all needed |
| **This name is already taken** | Change the web name to something else |
| **We couldn't check this name** | The form could not reach the network to check the web name. You can keep going. The name is checked again when you tap **Create Garden** |
| **Add or clear the address you typed** | An address is still sitting in a team field. Tap **Add** to include it, or clear the field |
| **You're offline** | Reconnect, then tap **Create Garden** again. What you typed is still there |
| **Not ready to create yet** | The form does not think it is complete. Check every field in section 01, then try again. If every field is filled and it still appears, tell the Green Goods team |
| **Transaction canceled** | You closed the confirmation. Nothing was created. Tap **Try Again** when you are ready |
| **Waiting for your wallet** | Your wallet accepted the request and it has not finished. Open your wallet and approve or finish it there, then tap **Check confirmation**. Do not create the garden a second time while this one is waiting |
| **Transaction failed** | Read the message under it, then tap **Try Again**. With your own wallet, the usual cause is not enough ETH for the fee |
| A person you added is not listed afterwards | Give the list a moment to catch up. If they are still missing, add them from [Manage Members](/community/steward-guide/managing-members) |

<Callout kind="note" title="Using your own wallet">

Creating a garden is one transaction on the Arbitrum network, so the account you create it from needs some ETH there. The Green Goods name fund pays for the garden's web name when it can. When it can't and your account has the ETH, the transaction includes that small fee too. Otherwise the garden is still created, and its web name follows once the fund can pay.

</Callout>
