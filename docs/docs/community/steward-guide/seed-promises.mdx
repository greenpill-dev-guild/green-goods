# Seed Promises

Prepare promises that people can understand and take up. Seeding can create one promise or a batch with different requirements.

## Before you start

- Sign in to [the steward dashboard](https://admin.greengoods.app) with access to the selected garden. [Get steward access](/community/steward-guide/#get-steward-access) if needed.
- Check the garden name at the top left, then open **Garden → Promises**. Read the target before signing.
- Have a steady connection for setup and direct changes. New money-reward promises, settlement configuration and payouts are deferred.
- Open the intended pool and season or campaign first.
- Agree the action counts, deadline, team policy and independent confirmers.

## Steps

Choose **Seed Promises**. As an illustrative example, your garden can request seedling-care sessions with a required planting action and a named independent reviewer.

1. In **What**, choose whether the pool offers help or requests it, the kind of promise, its title, and its season or campaign.
2. In **How Much**, set how many promises to create and what each asks for. For garden work, choose the required action rows and counts. Check the team policy and deadline.
3. In **Proof & Confirmation**, choose who can confirm, how many confirmations are needed, and whether taking it up needs steward approval. The provider and contributing team cannot confirm their own promise. A named group must have enough independent people to meet its threshold.
4. Leave **Does this come with a reward?** at **No** for this release. A displayed old reward or active contract is not permission to create a new money reward.
5. In **Review**, check each answer. Use **Add Another** if preparing different promises together, then choose **Create Promise** or **Create … Promises**, as shown.
6. Read the result for every promise. A created or queued item is not the same as one recorded by the shared system. Return to **Promises** and use **Send Now** only for the row whose status asks for it.

**Success:** the intended promises appear in the pool with the right terms and current status. A partly completed batch stays partly completed; do not recreate its successful rows.

## How you know it worked

Each intended promise appears with the correct terms and a result showing whether it has reached the shared record. Queued is not recorded.

## What happens next

Members take promises up directly or ask for steward approval. Share [Keep a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/keep-a-promise) with them.

## If something goes wrong

After an interrupted seed batch, reload the season and compare its promises with your intended list. Keep the records already created and send only the missing entries.

For a queued promise, return to **Promises** and use **Send Now** only for the row whose status asks for it. Do not recreate successful rows. Leave the reward choice at **No** for this release.

For an app problem, open **Settings → Support** in the dashboard. On a phone, find **Settings** under **Profile**. Share the affected record's link.
