# Seed Promises

Prepare promises that people can understand and take up. Seeding can create one promise or a batch with different requirements.

## Before you start

- Sign in to [the steward dashboard](https://admin.greengoods.app) with access to the selected garden. [Get steward access](/community/steward-guide/#get-steward-access) if needed.
- Check the garden name at the top left, then open **Garden → Promises**. Read the target before signing.
- Have a steady connection for setup and direct changes. New money-reward promises, settlement configuration and payouts are deferred.
- Open the intended pool and season or campaign first.
- Agree the action counts, deadline, team policy and independent confirmers.

## Steps

Choose **Seed Promises**. As an illustrative example, your garden can request seedling-care sessions with a required planting action and a named independent reviewer.

1. In **What**, choose whether the pool offers help or requests it, the kind of promise, its title, and its season or campaign.
2. In **How Much**, set how many promises to create and what each asks for. For garden work, choose the required action rows and counts. Check the team policy and deadline.
3. In **Proof & Confirmation**, choose who can confirm, how many confirmations are needed, and whether taking it up needs steward approval. The provider and contributing team cannot confirm their own promise. A named group must have enough independent people to meet its threshold. **Let the Green Goods team confirm if nobody local is eligible** starts on when the Green Goods team's pool is registered. The team can use it only while nobody local can confirm, always with a recorded reason. If the dashboard says it is unavailable, the promise is created with it off.
4. Expect no reward question. Money rewards are switched off in this release, so the form creates promises without one and **Review** shows the reward as **None**. Seeding from an older promise that carried a reward leaves the reward out; the original promise is unchanged.
5. In **Review**, check each answer. Use **Add Another** if preparing different promises together, then choose **Create Promise** or **Create … Promises**, as shown.

6. Read the result for every promise, then return to **Promises**. A group can show both **created** copies and copies that **didn’t send**. Keep the created copies and use **Finish Creating (n)** to send the rest. An individual queued row offers **Send Now** or **Try Again** instead.

**Success:** the intended promises appear in the pool with the right terms and current status. A partly completed batch stays partly completed; do not recreate its successful rows.

## How you know it worked

Each intended promise appears with the correct terms and a result showing whether it has reached the shared record. Queued is not recorded.

## What happens next

Members take promises up directly or ask for steward approval. Share [Keep a Promise](/community/gardener-guide/keep-a-promise) with them.

## If something goes wrong

After an interrupted seed batch, return to **Promises** on the same device and compare the group with your intended list:

- **n created** counts the copies already published to the shared record.
- **n didn’t send** counts copies still waiting in your queue. Choose **Finish Creating (n)** on that group to send those copies. They appear within the group rather than as separate queued rows.
- An individual queued promise has its own **Send Now** or **Try Again** control. Read its status and any recorded transaction before retrying.

For example, if a group shows **3 created** and **2 didn’t send**, choose **Finish Creating (2)**. Do not seed five new copies or recreate the three successful ones.

For an app problem, open **Settings → Support** in the dashboard. On a phone, find **Settings** under **Profile**. Share the affected record's link.
