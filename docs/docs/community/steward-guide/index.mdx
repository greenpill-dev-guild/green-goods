# Steward and Evaluator Guide

Stewards operate the Garden and record approval or rejection of submitted Work. Evaluators can help author or strengthen Assessments, methods, and impact claims; they do not approve Work.

- [Create a Garden](/community/steward-guide/creating-a-garden) when you have deployer authority.
- [Manage Actions](/community/steward-guide/managing-actions) to define the work Gardeners can document.
- [Create an Assessment](/community/steward-guide/making-an-assessment) to record baseline, outcome, and reporting context.
- [Review Work](/community/steward-guide/reviewing-work) as a steward and leave actionable feedback.
- [Mint an Impact Certificate](/community/steward-guide/creating-impact-certificates) only after the evidence chain is ready.
- [Manage Funding and Governance](/community/steward-guide/funding-and-governance) through the mechanism that fits the decision.

Check the selected Garden, account, role, and network before any write action. A visible route does not by itself grant permission.
