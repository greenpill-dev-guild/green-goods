# Steward Guide

A steward looks after a garden. You bring people in, make sure the right kinds of work can be recorded, review what gardeners submit, and look after the garden's shared funds. Your review is what turns a gardener's photos into a record other people can trust.

What you get:

- **One place to run the garden.** Members, submissions, assessments, and funds sit together in the steward cockpit.
- **A clear queue.** Work waits for you in one list, and nothing is approved without a person deciding.
- **A record that outlasts you.** Every decision is saved with your reason, so the next steward can see what happened and why.

## Where you work

- **The steward cockpit**, at [admin.greengoods.app](https://admin.greengoods.app). It is best on a computer. The bar at the bottom holds its workspaces: **Hub** for reviews and records, **Garden** for the garden's profile and health, and **Community** for members and funds.
- **The Green Goods app**, on your phone. You can review work there too, which helps when you are at the garden.

Before you change anything, look at the garden name at the top left of the cockpit. Everything you do applies to that garden.

## Get steward access

You need access to at least one garden before the cockpit opens. There are two ways in: ask from inside the cockpit, or have someone who already runs the garden add you.

### Before you start

- **Use the account you use in the app.** Access belongs to an account. A passkey account and a wallet are two different accounts.
- **Know the garden's name**, or have a link to it.

### Steps

1. Open [admin.greengoods.app](https://admin.greengoods.app).
2. Sign in. To use your passkey, tap **Already have an account?**, enter your account name, and tap **Sign in with Passkey**. To use a wallet, tap **Connect Wallet**. If you are new, tap **Create Account**.
3. On the **No garden access yet** screen, tap **Request Steward Access**.
4. Search for the garden by name, or paste its link or address, and pick it.
5. Under **Review Your Request**, add a short note about how you plan to help. It is optional, and it helps the person deciding.
6. Tap **Send Request** and sign the message. This is a signature, not a payment.

**Prefer to be added directly?** Open your profile in the cockpit, copy your account address, and send it to a garden owner or steward. They can add you from [Manage Members](/community/steward-guide/managing-members).

### How you know it worked

The request shows **Pending review**. Tap **View Steward Request** at any time to see it, **Check Status** to refresh it, or **Withdraw** to cancel it.

### What happens next

A garden owner or steward reviews your request in their cockpit and assigns the role. When they do, the request reads **Steward access confirmed** and the garden opens for you. Sending a request does not give access by itself.

### If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **Steward requests are unavailable right now** | Requests are switched off for the moment. Copy your account address from the same screen and send it to a garden owner or steward |
| **Wallet required** | You signed in with an email or social login, which the cockpit does not accept. Tap **Sign Out & Connect Wallet**, then use a passkey or a wallet |
| **No steward request was found for this account and garden** | You are signed in to a different account than the one you asked with. Sign in with that one |
| **Request declined** | Read the reason shown beside it. You can ask again when the garden allows a new request |
| **The request may have been saved** | Tap **Check Status** before you send another one |
| **Can't load garden access** | A temporary problem reading the gardens. Tap **Try Again** in a moment |

## Roles in a garden

Six roles exist in every garden. A person can hold more than one.

| Role | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Owner** | Everything a steward can do, plus renaming the garden and pausing its endowment in an emergency |
| **Steward** | Running the garden: members, settings, reviewing work, certificates, and funds |
| **Evaluator** | Writing assessments. An evaluator cannot approve or reject work |
| **Gardener** | Submitting work, and claiming from the garden's shared fund |
| **Funder** | Marks a supporter of the garden. It does not open the cockpit |
| **Community member** | Marks someone in the garden's wider community. It does not open the cockpit |

Giving someone the Steward role also gives them the Evaluator and Gardener roles. Giving someone Owner also gives them Steward.

## The jobs, in order

1. [Create a Garden](/community/steward-guide/creating-a-garden). Set up a new garden and its profile. Creating one needs extra permission.
2. [Manage Members](/community/steward-guide/managing-members). Add people, change roles, and answer requests to join.
3. [Manage Actions](/community/steward-guide/managing-actions). Make sure gardeners see the right kinds of work.
4. [Make an Assessment](/community/evaluator-guide/making-an-assessment). Write down the starting point and the goals before the work begins.
5. [Review Work](/community/steward-guide/reviewing-work). Approve good evidence, and reject with a reason that helps.
6. [Run a Commitment Pool](/community/steward-guide/commitment-pooling). Set up and look after the garden's promises.
7. [Mint an Impact Certificate](/community/steward-guide/creating-impact-certificates). Bundle approved work into one claim.
8. [Funding and Governance](/community/steward-guide/funding-and-governance). Look after the garden's shared fund and endowment.

:::note Using your own wallet
If you signed in with a passkey, Green Goods covers the network fees. If you connected your own wallet, each change is a transaction you pay for on the Arbitrum network, so keep a little ETH there.
:::
