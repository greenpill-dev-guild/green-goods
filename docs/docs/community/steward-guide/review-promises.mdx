# Review Promises

Keep each promise moving by reviewing participation and evidence. Taking someone onto a promise, approving their work, counting it, and confirming the promise are separate decisions.

## Before you start

- Sign in to [the steward dashboard](https://admin.greengoods.app) with access to the selected garden. [Get steward access](/community/steward-guide/#get-steward-access) if needed.
- Check the garden name at the top left, then open **Garden → Promises**. Read the target before signing.
- Have a steady connection for setup and direct changes. New money-reward promises, settlement configuration and payouts are deferred.
- Check the promise's terms, provider, team, requirements and confirmation rules. A contributing steward cannot confirm their own promise.

## Steps

### Review who takes it up

Open **Review Promises** in the pool. For each waiting request, check the promise, the account or garden taking it up, and any reason. Approving one person closes competing requests for that same promise. Choose **Approve** or decline the request once you have checked these details.

Acceptance sends directly when you choose **Approve** and confirm any signing prompt. Check the result before acting again. A request from a steward on behalf of another garden names that garden as the provider; the person who sent the request is a separate identity.

The pool may also allow eligible members to take promises up directly. Those do not need a separate participation decision from you.

### Count work and check confirmation

**Before readiness, check the team and contributions.** Readiness freezes them. Nobody on that team may confirm the promise, even if they also have a steward role.

For **garden work**, use the existing [Review Work guide](/community/steward-guide/reviewing-work) to review the linked submission. A work item can be linked and approved without its approval being counted toward the promise yet. If it says **Approved · waiting for a steward to count it**:

1. In **Garden → Promises**, open the affected promise and inspect its requirements and **Recovery** section.
2. When **Count Approved Linked Work** shows approved links waiting, choose **Count Linked Work**.
3. Read the named promise and the warning that counting can fix the team, then confirm **Count Linked Work**.
4. Wait for **Approved linked work counted.** If it reads **Waiting for indexed confirmation…**, check that result before retrying. If a newer work decision needs review, return to the work record first. Do not ask the gardener to upload a duplicate.

  <figcaption>An approval can still be waiting to count toward the promise.</figcaption>
</figure>

If the promise requires an assessment, ask an evaluator or steward to [make an assessment](/community/evaluator-guide/making-an-assessment) for the provider garden. Before readiness, choose **Attach Assessment…** when offered, then review **Attach an Assessment** and choose the current assessment that applies. If none is listed, record it for that provider garden first; an assessment from another garden does not fill the requirement.

Counted work that meets all required rows, together with any required assessment, can make the promise ready for confirmation automatically. Do not use **Send for Confirmation** as the garden-work readiness step. Other supported proof flows have their own send-for-confirmation action.

For confirmation:

1. Open **Hub → Confirm** and check the selected garden in the header. It scopes your authority; a row can concern another garden's pool if this garden has permission to confirm it. Each row says **Ready to confirm**, **Needs a steward step-in** or **Needs the Green Goods team**.
2. Open **Confirm Kept…** and read the target, provider, evidence, and confirmation count.
3. Confirm only if the promise was kept and you are eligible. Your confirmation cannot be undone. It may be one of several required; watch the count after it arrives. An ordinary confirmation can wait on this device until you are online; a dispute or a fallback needs a connection.

### When a promise stalls or is contested

Open the promise in **Garden → Promises**. The controls below appear only when the promise's state and your role allow them, and each one needs a connection. All except **Expire Now…** ask for a reason. The member's timeline shows it, except for a dispute: members see only that the promise is under review.

| Situation | Use | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Nobody on the ordinary path can still confirm it | **Confirm as Garden Fallback…** | You confirm it as a steward who is not on the team. The member sees that a garden steward confirmed it as a fallback, with your reason |
| Nobody local can confirm it, and the promise lets the Green Goods team step in | **Confirm for Green Goods Team…** | Only stewards of the Green Goods team's garden see this. It is recorded as the team's fallback, with a reason |
| The recipient cannot send it for confirmation, or required work has stalled | **Mark Ready…** | **Mark Ready with Override** makes it ready for confirmation. Work already counted still counts. It needs at least one counted contribution, an open season if it belongs to one, and someone who can still confirm. Readiness fixes the team |
| It was called off after someone took it up, before it was ready for confirmation | **Cancel Promise…** | **Cancel This Promise** records why and releases what the promise held. The member sees the reason |
| It is past its due date | **Expire Now…** | **Expire This Promise** is final. Waiting requests close and what the pool held for it is released. A due date alone does not expire a promise |
| Delivery is contested or something looks wrong | **Raise Dispute…**, or **Not yet…** in **Hub → Confirm** | Confirmation stops while stewards review it. Members see **Under review by stewards** |
| A review needs a decision | **Resolve…** | Choose **Restore Previous State**, **Kept**, **Canceled** or **Expired**. **Kept** counts as confirmed and fixes the team; a steward on the team cannot choose it, and neither can anyone for a promise that had expired |

A fallback or an override is never a way to confirm your own contribution. While a pool is paused, take-up and confirmation wait, but cancel, expire, dispute and resolve still work. Protocol-wide coordination lives in **Community → Coordination** and is outside this garden runbook.

## How you know it worked

The participation request has a recorded decision. Required work is counted, any required provider-garden assessment is attached, and the confirmation count reflects valid decisions.

## What happens next

Eligible confirmers act once the promise is ready. Readiness fixes the team and contributions. After all promises finish, the season can be ended.

## If something goes wrong

After an uncertain review transaction, reopen that promise and read its current status and history. Retry only a decision that has not already reached the record.

If work is approved but the promise is waiting, inspect the exact requirement, counting status, other required work and assessment. An assessment from another garden does not fill the requirement.

If confirmation is unavailable, check the selected garden, threshold and your eligibility. Fallback does not override self-confirmation exclusions, and it appears only once nobody on the ordinary path can confirm. If a dispute, cancellation or expiry has an uncertain result, reload the promise and read its state and timeline before acting again.

For an app problem, open **Settings → Support** in the dashboard. On a phone, find **Settings** under **Profile**. Share the affected record's link.
