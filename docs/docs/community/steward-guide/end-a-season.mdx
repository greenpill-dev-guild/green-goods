# End a Season

End a completed season or campaign to preserve its shared result. Reconciliation and archiving are separate actions.

## Before you start

- Sign in to [the steward dashboard](https://admin.greengoods.app) with access to the selected garden. [Get steward access](/community/steward-guide/#get-steward-access) if needed.
- Check the garden name at the top left, then open **Garden → Promises**. Read the target before signing.
- Have a steady connection for setup and direct changes. New money-reward promises, settlement configuration and payouts are deferred.
- Check all commitments in the period. Passing the end date does not finish them.
- Decide whether the garden wants an impact certificate before considering archive.

## Steps

1. Check that every commitment in the season has finished. If the page says some are still live, follow those records first. The end date alone does not finish them.
2. Choose **End Season…** or **End Campaign…**, check the named target, and confirm while online. The record becomes **Reconciled** and stops accepting commitments in that period.
3. If the garden wants an impact certificate and certificate creation is available, [make it while the season is reconciled](/community/steward-guide/creating-impact-certificates).
4. Only then consider **Archive…**. Read **Archive This Season** or **Archive This Campaign** before confirming. Archiving is final and prevents making a certificate from that cycle later; its history remains readable.

## How you know it worked

The ended period reads **Reconciled** and stops accepting commitments. If you later archive it, its history remains readable but the archive decision is final.

## What happens next

Create an impact certificate while the season is reconciled, if that action is available. Archiving first prevents creating a certificate from that cycle later. A kept promise or reconciled period is not a payout receipt.

## If something goes wrong

If closing or archiving has an uncertain result, reload the season and check its state before acting again. Archive only after the required review is complete; it cannot be undone.

If live commitments remain, follow those records first. Do not open duplicate seasons to bypass the existing period. Money-reward and settlement operations remain deferred.

For an app problem, open **Settings → Support** in the dashboard. On a phone, find **Settings** under **Profile**. Share the affected record's link.
