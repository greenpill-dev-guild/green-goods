# Product History

Green Goods began with recording conservation work and grew into a way to connect a community's work, review, and funding. This timeline explains the changes people using it would notice.

The published release notes below are the evidence for released versions. Early specifications describe the direction at the time. For instructions that match the current product, start with [How It Works](/community/how-it-works).

## v0.1: recording conservation work {#v01}

**Early specification.** The first design focused on a gardener choosing an action, documenting what they did, and having it reviewed. Garden organizers would bring people and resources together. It explored how recognition and rewards might follow that work; those ideas were not a guarantee of payment.

The [archived specification](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/blob/v1.1.0/docs/docs/builders/specs/v0-1.mdx) records that starting point. It is not a release receipt or today's sign-in guide.

## v0.4: a garden's work record {#v04}

**January 2026 release.** Gardeners could record work with media and follow submissions in the app. Stewards could review evidence. The conservation focus included invasive-species removal, planting, and tending plants.

The [v0.4.0 release commit](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/commit/8ba29dbbe6db2ca556e5cb2030d70e297b0e6aad) records this version; the later [v1.1 release summary](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/pull/509) identifies it as the previous production release. The [v0.4 specification](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/blob/v1.1.0/docs/docs/builders/specs/v0-4.mdx) also explored impact reporting. Its proposed features should not all be read as shipped.

## v1.0: connecting work and funding {#v10}

**Product design milestone.** The scope widened beyond biodiversity to Solar, Agroforestry, Education, and Waste. The design connected gardener submissions with assessments, impact certificates, shared garden funds, and endowments. It also explored community voting and reputation.

These were separate pieces of work, with separate delivery dates. The [v1.0 specification](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/blob/v1.1.0/docs/docs/builders/specs/v1-0.mdx) is historical context, not evidence that every part became available at once.

## v1.1.0: clearer places to work {#v110}

**May 2026 release milestone.** Gardeners gained a refreshed public website and installed-app experience. The public garden view brought evidence and source records together. Stewards gained a redesigned dashboard, including a member roster and clearer navigation. Repeated messaging callbacks were made safer to retry.

The [merged release summary](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/pull/509) and [version tag](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/tree/v1.1.0) record this milestone. Its historical screenshots and labels may differ from the guides here.

## v1.2.0: steadier recording and funding {#v120}

**Released 8 July 2026.** Installation and update messages became clearer. Work-media submission and stalled uploads gained recovery fixes. The funding page clarified the difference between donating to a shared fund and making an endowment; the endowment flow gained a currency display choice. Steward workflows and member management received further improvements.

Use the current [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating), [Recover Unsent Work](/community/gardener-guide/recovery-and-sync), and [Funder Guide](/community/funder-guide/) for today's steps. [Release notes](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/releases/tag/v1.2.0).

## v1.2.1: recovering from app updates {#v121}

**Released 30 July 2026.** A startup failure could show a recovery screen with **Reload**, rather than leaving the screen blank. App updates preserved open tabs, and some failures loading a new version recovered with one automatic reload. Passkey sign-in was enabled by default in production.

This version also had a passkey signing fault fixed in v1.2.2. Signing in and approving an action were different outcomes: sign-in could succeed while the action failed. [Release notes](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/releases/tag/v1.2.1).

## v1.2.2: passkey actions work again {#v122}

**Released 12 September 2026.** People signing in with a passkey could approve actions again, including joining a garden and submitting work. The fix kept existing account addresses and passkeys: there was no need to create another account or register another passkey.

If an old app version is still open, follow [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating). [Release notes](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/releases/tag/v1.2.2).

## v2.0.0: work and promises {#v200}

**Next major version; production release not verified here.** The planned direction connects Commitment Pooling, promises, work evidence, and confirmation across the gardener app, steward dashboard, and public garden records. Read [where pooling fits](/community/how-it-works#commitment-pooling) for the idea.

A release schedule does not establish that a feature is live. The latest published release found for this page on 12 October 2026 was v1.2.2. Pooling payment and settlement need their own activation evidence; this page does not promise automatic payment or ask existing users to migrate accounts.

## Historical research

These proposals explored possible directions. They are not extra released versions or instructions to change your account. The [historical source files](https://github.com/greenpill-dev-guild/green-goods/tree/v1.1.0/docs/docs/builders/specs) remain available.

### Identity interoperability {#did-ethr-modernization}

A March 2026 proposal explored identity interoperability and privacy. It was research, not an adopted implementation plan.

### Reputation and impact measurement {#greenwill-research}

March 2026 proposals explored reputation badges and impact measurement. Individual design and delivery decisions followed separately.

### Credential-based access {#opencred-vc-login}

A March 2026 proposal explored using credentials for geographic garden access and account linking. It was not adopted as an active implementation plan.

### Passkey recovery research {#passkey-recovery-hardening}

A March 2026 proposal explored account recovery and access from a second device. For current limits, read [Install and Update](/community/gardener-guide/installing-and-updating#if-something-goes-wrong).
