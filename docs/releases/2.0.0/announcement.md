## Telegram and WhatsApp

Green Goods 2.0 is here 🌱

This release brings your Garden's promises, documented work, and review closer together.

There's a rebuilt steward workspace, clearer ways to request Garden access, and better
controls for offline work and app updates.

Already part of a Garden? Update the app, give it a try, and tell us what feels easier,
or where you get stuck.

We're still building closely with pilot communities. If your community would like to try
Green Goods, reply here or message us. We'd love to hear what you're working on.

Thank you to everyone testing and shaping this with us 💚

https://greengoods.app

G$ settlement and contributor payouts are planned for a later release.
