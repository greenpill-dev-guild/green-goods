# Prototype participants and gardens — scope decision

**Date:** 25 September 2026. **Authority:** user confirmation following the architecture review.

The demo uses existing wallets and pre-enrolled participants. TAS is the initial community;
Aiyeloja Family Garden is also a prototype garden. Account creation and garden enrollment are
outside this demo. Linking an existing account to WhatsApp and recovering that link remain included.

A DM selects or confirms its garden, and each draft retains that garden. TAS membership does not
confer membership in Aiyeloja Family Garden. Enable a garden only after its Arbitrum address,
active Action, reporter membership and distinct reviewer authority are verified. No addresses or
live memberships were supplied or verified by this decision.

This closes the onboarding question in the [architecture review](2026-09-25-architecture-review.md).
That dated review remains unchanged; its runtime and deployment proof limits still apply.
