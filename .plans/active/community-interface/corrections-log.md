**Survey transposition pass.** While transposing into Forms, the owner surfaced ~20 issues; the instrument was rebuilt as a paste-ready build sheet (48 main-path items). Two accuracy corrections came out of checking rather than the feedback itself:

- **The work grid was wrong.** It listed Planting / Watering / Identify Plant / Solar Hub Session / Web3 Workshop / Farm Registration, taken from `v1-0.mdx` rather than the product. The live registry (`packages/contracts/config/actions.json`) holds 23 actions — SOLAR 5, AGRO 6, EDU 5, WASTE 7 — and the grid is now four per-domain checkbox grids generated from it. ⚠️ `packages/shared/src/types/domain.ts:365` says "all 22 actions", off by one against the config; worth reconciling.
- **Season copy was contradictory.** The glossary says "typically a quarter", but `usePublicVolume.ts:38-50` shows Season One open-ended since 2025-04-01 (~15 months) with no on-chain Season primitive. Survey copy now hedges: "usually about three months, though our first Season has run longer while everyone got started."

**Standing architecture finding — metrics do not accumulate into baselines.** Work submissions capture `timeSpentMinutes` plus typed per-action details (units kg, kWh, min, hours) and persist them to IPFS/EAS, but nothing aggregates them: there is **no Work entity in `packages/indexer/schema.graphql`**, `details` is an untyped `Record<string, unknown>` with no metric registry, and hypercert bundling filters records by action and reporting window rather than totalling their values. `SmartOutcome.metric`/`target` are operator-authored targets, not derived actuals. Consequence: assessment deltas are computed by hand, and an assessment cannot yet be informed by the work already recorded against a garden. The owner has flagged this as something to architect at a higher level; Section 4 of the survey is deliberately shaped to gather evidence for that design. **Tracked as [PRD-719](https://linear.app/greenpill-dev-guild/issue/PRD-719/assessment-deltas-from-accumulated-work-metrics) — "Assessment deltas from accumulated work metrics"** (Product · Green Goods Impact Reporting & Public Metrics · `activity:architecture`), opened 2026-07-18. Its first deliverable is an ADR answering six design questions, the load-bearing one being whether the indexer boundary is amended to carry work metrics. COM-7 Section 4 responses are a named input, so the metric registry is designed against what gardens actually track rather than in the abstract.
