## What Pass 2 is

A 60-minute working call (book 75) that turns a completed survey response into action: correct and confirm the garden's mandate, agree how change gets measured, plan who gets onboarded onto Green Goods, walk the first-Season timeline, and name support. Run the survey first — this call assumes a completed response.

The steward should leave knowing exactly what happens next and why it is worth it. "Not ready" is a respected answer and is recorded as such.

## Where the call's outputs are recorded
