## Purpose & goals

The survey is Pass 1 of the two-pass onboarding instrument: a ~20-minute self-serve form each cohort garden completes **before** its onboarding call, so the call can be about action — confirming the mandate, setting the first-Season timeline, and walking the incentives — instead of discovery. It walks a garden from its needs, to the promises that could address them, to how kept promises will be measured, and it should leave the person filling it out with more clarity about their own garden's pattern than they started with.

Each section produces evidence for one of the six research decisions:

1. **Seeding go/no-go per garden** — a mandate artifact each garden recognizes as its own plan; decides which gardens enter operator-curated seeding 2026-08-31.
2. **Ship-or-revise the needs vocabulary** — whether Request/Offer/Initiative, the need→desired-outcome pairing, horizon chips, and stage-of-clarity language carry each garden's reality; the "Other"/misfit answers are the architecture-gap instrument.
3. **Which commitment units seed Cycle 1** — registry fit, missing work types, capacity and support bounds.
4. **Whether mutual credit earns a design lane** — existing member-to-member exchange patterns plus lightly captured credit terms.
5. **What anchors measurement** — what each garden tracks, wants to track, and must report to funders; decides assessment templates and baseline authorship (absorbs canceled RESR-53).
6. **G$ rollout order** — per-garden settlement relevance and a real local circulation path.

Coordination-channel and join-flow observations feed RESR-64 (join-request persistence, due 2026-08-12) — not an outcome of this survey.

**Cohort.** Named focus candidates are Tech and Sun Hub (Awka, Nigeria — English), Greenpill Cape Town (Muizenberg / Deep South Circles — English; UNICEF-funded trash-bin/waste-cleanup program; showcase folded in from canceled RESR-53), and AgroforestDAO / Redemption Hill (Bias Fortes, Brazil — Portuguese), plus a fourth garden — in outreach, named only once participation is confirmed — for the mature MRV-adoption slot selected against the RESR-58 criteria. Naming a candidate does not presume readiness.

**A garden enters fielding only when all are named:**

- operator contact and backup contact;
- preferred language and accessible interview format;
- community consent path for notes/audio/photos;
- likely gathering date or remote session;
- one plausible need/offer/initiative and one commitment candidate;
- whether G$ settlement is relevant for this garden now;
- interviewer, note-taker, and follow-up owner.

**Language.** This document is the English master. One Google Form per language: Portuguese is required for Bias Fortes, and the fourth-garden language is confirmed privately before its translation is prepared. Translation is a **fielding gate** — translate after the English questions settle and before a garden receives the link.

**Build notes (Google Forms).**

- One Form; sections below map 1:1 to Forms sections. Progress bar on.
- Q4.6 uses **"Go to section based on answer"** for the 4A/4B branches (Forms cannot branch on earlier answers later, which is why the router is a question, not garden-picker logic).

- Confirmation message = the Section 6 outro copy.
- **Totals**: 45 questions main path (32 required / 13 optional); +4 (UNICEF branch) or +5 (methodology branch); ~69% closed with "Other" escapes; estimated 18–24 minutes. **Cut ladder if piloting shows >25 min**: 3.11 + 3.12 first, then 4.2, then 1.6. ## Privacy & consent

- **Three tiers, explained to participants**: nothing from this survey goes on-chain or public; responses live in restricted research-notes storage (the Form's response sheet + call notes); anything retracted is deleted.
- Consent is **separate per use** — written notes (5.5), quotations (5.6), photos/audio (5.7) — and recorded as enumerated values only.
- A **no-recording path** exists for the call, and any paragraph answer can arrive as a voice note to the support contact instead of typed text.
- Participants may **retract research notes any time before synthesis** by telling the support contact.
- Hard boundary: grievances naming individuals, wallet addresses, join-request identities, and contact details never enter Linear, PostHog, or public artifacts. Analytics stores enumerated/boolean research metadata only. Raw response rows are never pasted into Linear.

## Synthesis

- **Draft mandate rows** (1–3 per garden), assembled by the facilitator before the call from: 3.4 (who/what/for whom/by when) + 2.2/2.4/2.5 (need, desired outcome, horizon) + 3.3 (domains) + 3.1 (unit candidates) + 3.5 (quantity ceiling) + 4.1/4.5 (evidence, baseline author) + 1.5 (confirmation-group scale). The Pass 2 call (`onboarding-call.md`) corrects and confirms them.
- **Per-garden readiness row**: Participation (confirmed / invited / unavailable — never inferred) · Need examples (in the community's language) · Commitment rows (confirmed vs draft, clearly separated) · Domains (optional; empty is valid) · Evidence/confirmation (named and feasible) · G$ settlement (relevant now / later / not discussed; Celo only) · Local circulation (known sink, unknown, or n/a) · Open decisions (owner and next review point).
- **Gap log** (the architecture-gap instrument): every "I'm not sure it fits" (2.3), horizon Other (2.5), "somewhere else" domain (3.3), off-registry work (3.2), and 6.3 answer — routed to architecture review before August seeding.
- **Instrument-efficacy pulse**: 6.1 read per garden to size call time and test whether the survey built clarity; never aggregated into a score.
- **No cross-garden leaderboard, readiness score, or false completeness percentage.** Scales are self-reports read within a garden only.
