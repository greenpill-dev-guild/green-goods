## What Pass 1 is

A self-serve survey each cohort garden completes before its onboarding call, so the call can be about action — confirming the mandate, planning platform onboarding, setting the first-Season timeline — instead of discovery. Five sections plus a generalized funder/programme branch; 20–26 minutes.

Two framing rules the instrument exists to hold:

- **Section 2 is about needs only.** A need is something a community is trying to solve, never a direction of help. Nothing narrows to a single need.
- **Request and Offer are commitment direction (3.7)**, not a property of a need — that is the layer where the distinction sets who confirms a commitment was kept.

Section 3's work grids are generated from the live action registry (`packages/contracts/config/actions.json`), so registry fit is measured against what the product actually records.

## Privacy

Responses live in restricted research-notes storage — the Form's response sheet plus call notes. **Raw response rows are never pasted into Linear.** Contact details, wallet addresses, join identities, and grievances naming individuals never enter Linear, analytics, or public artifacts, and never this repository. No cross-garden leaderboard, readiness score, or completeness percentage; scales are self-reports read within a garden only.
