# Garden membership dates

Gardeners and stewards need to distinguish the date someone joined a particular garden from their first gardener role anywhere. Dated membership facts also help stewards recognize accounts before managing their roles.

The user approved garden join, rejoin, and role dates in PWA member details, profile garden memberships, and Admin member details on November 11, 2026. Account removal is a separate operational action; private identities stay outside this public hub.

Success means the same recorded garden dates appear across the existing interfaces, unknown history stays unknown, and existing role permissions and signing paths are preserved.
