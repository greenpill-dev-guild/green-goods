# Garden membership dates evaluation

- First role in another garden does not affect this garden join date.
- Removal of one role while another remains does not create a rejoin.
- Last-role removal followed by grant retains first join and records the new start.
- Duplicate grants, duplicate event delivery, and reverse delivery preserve chronological facts.
- Every page is read; malformed or unavailable data returns no invented dates.
- Dates are scoped by chain, garden and account, and refresh with existing garden invalidation.
- PWA and Admin show localized facts and exact copyable account addresses.
- Verify loading, populated, rejoined and unavailable rendering, including a narrow viewport.
- Hosted schema publication and historical replay remain a release prerequisite.
