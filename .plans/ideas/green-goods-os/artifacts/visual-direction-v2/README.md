## Design authority and future image rule

Use the [root design system](../../../../../DESIGN.md), [browser dialect](../../../../../packages/client/DESIGN.browser.md), Fraunces/Inter, warm linen `#FAF8F5`, charcoal `#292524`, stone `#78716C` and restrained green `#1A7544`. The actual website references were inspected again before generation: [home](../../../../../packages/client/public/images/hero-home.webp), [actions](../../../../../packages/client/public/images/hero-actions.webp) and [cookie](../../../../../packages/client/public/images/hero-cookie.webp). They establish place, materials, tools and cropped activity.

**No AI-generated human faces or heads in deck imagery.** Check background figures, screens, photographs and reflections as well as the main subject. Prefer landscapes, practical infrastructure, tools, notebooks and cropped hands where they explain an activity. Keep images on the right of split compositions. Do not add fake product interfaces, invented metrics, testimonials or customer scenes.

The earlier [v1 checkpoint](../visual-direction/README.md) is historical. Its face-containing images and prompts are superseded and must not be reused.

Create a new photographic concept for Green Goods using the supplied hero-home.webp as the exact style reference: natural warm late-afternoon light, ordinary neighborhood garden, weathered wood, restrained olive and soil colors, tactile materials and believable depth. ### Recordkeeping and shared learning

Asset: [field-notebook-concept.png](field-notebook-concept.png). References: `hero-actions.webp` and `hero-cookie.webp`.

Original tool output: `/Users/afo/.codex/generated_images/01a11c2f-12db-7f32-b498-5e3e984c6030/exec-16ad90d0-3921-477e-a9a5-28eeea753b38.png`.

```text
Create a new photographic concept for Green Goods using the supplied website images as strict style/framing references. Reference 1 hero-actions.webp: tactile wooden table, field notebook, natural olive/cream/soil palette and warm daylight. Reference 2 hero-cookie.webp: human participation shown ONLY by cropped forearms and hands around shared materials, NEVER faces or heads.

The new scene shows practical stewardship recordkeeping and exchange: an open field notebook with rough non-legible plot sketches, a few printed PLANT photographs, pencil, and an ordinary used unbranded laptop on a weathered garden table. The laptop screen is neutral and at an oblique angle; no legible interface or chart. One forearm enters from the lower-left edge, hand pointing to the notebook; another enters from the top-right edge, hand holding a pencil over the page. Exactly two hands, natural anatomy. The camera looks steeply DOWN at the table, with a narrow edge of garden greenery visible. The frame ENDS at forearms. No head, face, neck, eyes, portrait, reflection of a person, or face in any photograph or screen can appear anywhere.

Horizontal 4:3 composition for the RIGHT SIDE of a slide. Put notebook and hands toward the image center, laptop off-center upper left, plants/tools around the edges. Keep essential action inside safe margins. Natural material texture, credible everyday scale, imperfect used paper and wood. Match the understated website photography. No painting, CGI, silhouettes, floating UI, diagram, logo, legible text, watermark, expensive tech, coins or tokens. This is a fictional conceptual scene, not a real customer or field result.
```
