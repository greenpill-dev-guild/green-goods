## 1. Cover, Fraunces Medium 500 with the accent green second line

Green Goods helps communities caring for land and each other access useful tools at a low cost, learn from one another and coordinate support while retaining sovereignty over their knowledge.

Our global vision is that every community, regardless of economic circumstances, can access the tools, infrastructure, knowledge and support to care for land and each other. Local language, governance, ability, connectivity and time matter alongside money. Green Goods is the product, protocol and ecosystem. The proposed OS is a workspace within that wider offer.

Affordability and practical sovereignty are commitments to deliver and test. They are not measured savings or a promise of control over copies already shared. The four agreed pillars are Accessibility, Sovereignty, Trust and Reciprocity, with Privacy, Interoperability and Verifiability essential throughout.
