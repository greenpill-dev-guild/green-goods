## Slide 1

Green Goods is the primary product, protocol and ecosystem. OS is a working vision; the first product should be called a community workspace. This deck proposes a bounded learning investment, not a launched OS or demonstrated business. Cover: AI-generated conceptual nursery illustration, built-in image generation, 2026-10-08. It depicts no real community, site or deployment. The illustration is replaceable; text throughout the deck is editable. Full source register and research are supplied with this presentation.

## Slide 2

Problem hypothesis: notes, readings and practical experience can be difficult to retrieve and hand over. We have not measured how much time these users currently lose. The pilot must establish that baseline and compare a form/shared-folder workflow before claiming savings. The desired outcome is a better local decision; reporting to a supporter is a downstream reuse.

## Slide 5

The question is which nursery beds need a manual moisture check today. The first input is synthetic recording SM-01, with nominal moisture units and deliberately missing/stale/outlier rows. This does not test a physical sensor or establish agronomic thresholds. The model may draft explanations but may not prescribe irrigation, publish or sign. The Green Goods boundary is an unsubmitted export; the AT step is an authorized synthetic sandbox publication.

## Slide 6

These pillars are proposed for evaluation, not adopted doctrine. Affordability supports accessibility; control and portability support sovereignty; accountable review supports trust; useful exchange supports reciprocity. Privacy, interoperability and verifiability are requirements across all four. More replication can improve access while increasing exposure; transparency must be appropriate to the audience.

## Slide 9

The network hypothesis is reciprocal. Better-resourced stewards may offer funded curation, expertise or equipment support. Resource-constrained communities may offer valuable local practice and context, without an obligation to disclose sensitive knowledge. Match conditions before transferring a method. The pilot tests exchanges in both directions, practical usefulness, attribution, refusal and review effort. Cultural and experiential value do not need token prices.
