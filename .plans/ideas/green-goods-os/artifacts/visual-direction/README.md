## Direction and references

Use the public website's editorial language: Fraunces headings, Inter body, warm linen `#FAF8F5`, charcoal `#292524`, stone `#78716C` and restrained green `#1A7544`. New photography-like concepts should retain the website's natural light, weathered materials, human scale and visible garden context. No rejected painterly landscape, generic futuristic network, invented statistic or product screenshot.

### Community care

Output: [community-care-concept.png](community-care-concept.png)

Style references: `packages/client/public/images/hero-home.webp` and `hero-garden.webp`.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a NEW photographic concept for a Green Goods pitch cover, using the two supplied website images as style references only. Do not reproduce their exact people or location.

Reference 1 hero-home.webp: match its cinematic natural light, tactile weathered materials, grounded community-garden context and restrained olive, soil-brown and warm-cream colors. Reference 2 hero-garden.webp: match human scale, believable everyday work and natural photographic texture.

New scene: an ordinary community growing space at the edge of a lived-in neighborhood, early evening. A path threads through established vegetable beds, fruit trees and a modest timber tool shelter with a small practical rainwater tank and solar panel. In the RIGHT THIRD of the frame, two adult land stewards of different ages work together checking a young fruit tree's support and mulch, naturally and without posing. A third adult walks toward them with a notebook in the middle distance. Practical clothing, lived-in tools, no poverty spectacle or luxury estate. No generic cupped hands holding a seedling.

Composition: horizontal 16:9 editorial photograph. Important people and actions concentrated between 65% and 90% of frame width. The left 53% will be covered by a solid linen title panel in the slide, so keep that region as believable soft garden context, with no essential subject. Ground-level 35mm camera, dimensional landscape context, realistic proportions and anatomy. Warm light without orange filters or hazy fantasy. Natural photographic detail, not gouache, watercolor, illustration, CGI, glossy stock imagery or oversaturated green. No text, logos, UI, diagrams, watermark or oversized technology. The image is a fictional concept, not a real Green Goods project.
```

### Shared learning

Output: [shared-learning-concept.png](shared-learning-concept.png)

Style references: `packages/client/public/images/hero-actions.webp` and `hero-garden.webp`.

Exact prompt:

```text
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a NEW editorial photographic concept for Green Goods, using these website images as visual references only. Image 1 hero-actions.webp establishes the natural warm light, weathered wood, field notebook and tactile learning materials. Image 2 hero-garden.webp establishes the practical human scale, olive and earth palette and lived-in growing space. Match this photographic language closely. Do not make an illustration or reproduce the exact reference scene.

Show two adult community land stewards at a shaded outdoor worktable beside their garden, comparing a field notebook with an ordinary older unbranded laptop. One person explains a small hand-drawn plot sketch in the notebook; the other listens and adds a note. Show their faces in partial profile with natural concentration, no posing for camera. Their hands must be anatomically believable. The open laptop has a dim neutral screen angled away; no legible UI, charts or product claims. A simple pencil, one small printed plant photograph and a well-used hand tool sit on the table. Keep the notebook, listening and exchange central. Garden beds, a modest fence and foliage remain visible in the background.

Horizontal 3:2 composition suitable for a landscape image occupying one half of an editorial slide. All essential people, hands, notebook and laptop within the central 85% for safe cropping. Eye-level three-quarter camera, not flat lay. Warm daylight with open shadows, restrained olive greens, cream cotton and timber browns. Plausible lens and depth of field, clear context and realistic skin/material texture. No luxury equipment, exposed electronic circuits, dashboards, fantasy network overlays, logos, legible text, watermark, tokens or coins. No painterly brushwork, CGI or stock-photo handshake. This is a fictional concept about sharing practical knowledge, not a record of a real pilot.
```
