## Decisions already accepted

Authoritative central proposition:

> Green Goods helps communities caring for land and each other access useful tools at a low cost, learn from one another and coordinate support while retaining sovereignty over their knowledge.

Green Goods is the product, protocol and ecosystem. OS is the proposed workspace within it; Coop is a reference project, not an adopted architecture or identity. The global vision serves communities regardless of economic circumstances. Land stewards is the umbrella; keep the four concrete settings: a mid-sized transitioning farmer, a small regenerative farmer, a community managing land in the Global South, and a well-resourced environmental steward. Treat all as potential contributors and learners.

The accepted pillars are Accessibility, Sovereignty, Trust and Reciprocity. Privacy, Interoperability and Verifiability remain essential across them. Community usefulness comes before funder reporting. Shared knowledge should preserve local context, attribution, consent and differences, and help relationships grow within and across bioregions. Financial, social, intellectual, cultural and experiential value are distinct; do not force them into tokens or equivalents.
