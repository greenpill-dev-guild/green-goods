# Impact Sharing and Report Exports

Gardeners and stewards need familiar ways to share public Work, commitments, assessments and
certificates, plus a report they can download and reuse. This track is independent of Certified
account linking and all provider grant negotiations.

Success means an approved versioned snapshot yields a public page/preview, honest user-driven
share outcomes and accessible deterministic exports. H1/H2 policy, hosting and composer proof
remain open. No social OAuth, automatic posting, provider publication or contract changes are
included. Creating the backlog does not activate runtime implementation.
