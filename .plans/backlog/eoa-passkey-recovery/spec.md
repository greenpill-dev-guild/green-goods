# EOA recovery for Kernel 0.3.3

## Eligible account and authority

The first cohort is a deployed Green Goods account on Arbitrum One with the pinned Kernel 0.3.3 implementation and supported WebAuthn root. Check the chain, implementation bytecode, current root and validator bytecode before offering enrollment or recovery. Treat existing pilot profiles and account proofs as separate compatibility cases. Establish the actual eligible cohort before enabling the feature.

The current passkey owner installs, replaces, or removes the recovery wallet. Enrollment proves that the member controls the EOA and shows the exact wallet and account being paired. The recovery wallet can authorize a new passkey for that account. Its authority to choose the key means it can gain full control after recovery. The app, directory, agent, garden stewards, and support have no recovery signing authority.

## Recovery lifecycle

1. **Enroll.** The owner signs enrollment while they still have a working passkey. Show recovery as available only after the receipt and onchain guardian state agree. Persisting a connected address in the app confers no authority.
2. **Prepare.** The member locates their existing account and creates a fresh passkey for the supported Green Goods RP. Bind the recovery authorization to the original chain and account, current enrollment generation, and exact replacement key and credential commitment. Show the recovery wallet what it is authorizing.
3. **Rotate.** A narrowly scoped executor checks the current guardian and approved account configuration, replaces the key atomically, consumes that enrollment, and emits the evidence needed to verify the change. A failed rotation preserves the old key and enrollment. The owner may remove or replace an enrollment before it executes. A stale, removed, or consumed enrollment cannot be replayed. The fork used the installed validator's key lifecycle within one atomic executor call and kept the Kernel root and implementation unchanged. Carry that design forward only after reviewing the deployed bytecode and proving its invariants. [Fork evidence](reports/2026-11-08-arbitrum-fork.md)
4. **Bind.** After the agreed Arbitrum confirmation rule, the passkey directory verifies the successful recovery receipt against the pinned executor and account and compares the replacement public key with the current validator state. It also verifies a fresh, single-use WebAuthn challenge for the replacement credential. The challenge binds the recovery purpose, original chain and address, and intended credential. Use a chain-backed recovery sequence to reject stale receipts even if a later rotation uses the same public key. Commit the verified binding and retire the previous directory credential in one transaction. Exact retries return the same binding. Failed RPC, reorged receipt, or a newer recovery leaves the request retryable or superseded without changing the address.
5. **Sign in.** The clean browser gets the verified binding from the directory and proves possession of the replacement passkey. Build the signing account at the verified original address. Check current onchain authority before using a recovered binding and reject stale cached credentials. Gate new sessions and signing from the superseded passkey on the surfaces that use this directory. Unrecovered accounts keep their existing derivation and sign-in. A chain where the account has not been recovered requires its own verified authority before the resolver offers signing there.

A wallet address, user name, browser storage entry, or transaction hash supplied by a caller is a lookup hint. A recovery proof earns the address binding. The existing name and directory identity stay with the original account. ENS reassignment stays in its own plan. ## Reporting grants after recovery

Passkey replacement leaves existing grants active. After sign-in, show a prompt to review active reporting grants and let the recovered owner remove selected grants or every positively identified reporting grant. A failed scan or incomplete history must be visible. The owner can retry without an Agent session or access to the old browser.

Use the account's onchain selector events and current permission state to discover candidates on 0.3.3. A candidate must match the allowlisted reporting signer and policy configuration, including the deployed guard, before it becomes removable in this flow. Verify the supported discovery window against actual grant issuance, including any installation paths and historical events in the cohort. Saved descriptors can aid recovery and must be independently verified. If a permission cannot be classified, leave it visible as unverified and keep its status out of the completed count. Preserve existing 0.3.1 behavior.

Use the recovered passkey to authorize targeted `uninstallValidation` calls with deinitialization data for the verified reporting policy stack. After receipt, read each selected permission and the guard state again. Show completion only when the selected grants are inactive and their guard permissions retired. Prove that a previously signed delegation or enable approval cannot restore a retired permission ID, and that the owner's ERC-1271 and UserOperations still work. A partial result or unknown receipt must remain reconcilable from chain state. The 0.3.3 flow must never fall back to a nonce sweep.

## Before rollout

Decided on 21 November 2026: recovery is delayed by 48 hours by default in the app and the current passkey can cancel a pending request; the chain is the authority for a recovered account and the directory keeps a re-checked pointer. Still to settle before production enrollment: owner notification, the eligible cohort, and the accepted Arbitrum confirmation and reorg policy. Confirm that recovery can finish and reporting permissions can be removed while new enrollment is paused. Release must cover the real owner and guardian journeys with the approved sponsorship policy and a usable fallback for temporary RPC, directory, or paymaster failure. A user who loses every credential before enrolling has no recovery route in this feature.
