# Community Needs & Signals

## Outcome

Give community members a low-barrier, garden-scoped way to name Needs, signal shared priorities, connect Needs to commitments and proof, and let operators moderate the record without turning funding into a ranking mechanic.

## Delivery shape

- A Needs substrate across contracts, indexer, shared state, sponsorship policy, and voice evidence.
- An independent Community PWA plus admin triage and a public funder lens.
- Explicit research gates for membership persistence, operator readiness, privacy, and pilot operations.
- Commitment Pooling integration through `needUID`, not a second settlement system.

## Boundaries

The hub excludes a v1 claim flow, per-Need escrow, funding-ranked boards, push notifications, and evidence-free promotion of parked hardening work. [spec.md](spec.md) owns the implementation contract; [plan.todo.md](plan.todo.md) owns sequencing.
