# Commitment Credit — August Companion Evaluation

## Acceptance gates

- The register is records-only and exposes no custody or transfer authority.
- Borrower caps, approval separation, installment repayment, default recovery, cancellation, pause, and upgrade/storage behavior have RED/GREEN contract proof.
- Rail references are verified under the approved chain-specific policy; a human-entered hash alone cannot count repayment.
- Indexer replay is idempotent and composite identifiers remain chain-safe.
- Admin/member states use non-shaming mutual-aid language and never expose a score or ranking.
- Deployment and live-rail proof remain separately authorized; local tests do not imply broadcast readiness.

## Proof limit

The existing specification is design evidence, not proof that the August companion is currently safe or ready to dispatch.
