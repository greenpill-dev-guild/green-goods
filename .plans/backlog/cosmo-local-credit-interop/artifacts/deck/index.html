We built commitment pooling: communities promise useful work, prove it, and turn the record into support. Today we show it at a solar hub in Nigeria, and how capital can follow and sustain the work.

Communities create value every season, but the record of it stays trapped.

Proving the work costs more than doing it.

So we built commitment pooling: promises a community can see, keep and fund.

Members offer or request specific work: a workshop, a repair, a planting.

Anyone in the garden can take a promise up, as themselves or for their garden.

Only the person it was made for can confirm it was kept.

Kept promises become a record that rewards and funding can follow.

3 / 16

It runs in four steps, season after season.

Needs

Stewards and evaluators record what the place needs, from steady power to new skills, and where things stand today.

Promise

Members promise specific work, like a workshop, a repair or a website, and agree who does what by when.

Work

The work is documented with photos and notes, reviewed by stewards and confirmed by the people it served.

Funding

Supporters fund what the record shows: the garden's shared fund now, or an endowment whose earnings support the work.

4 / 16

Five roles keep the loop honest.

  • Gardeners

    Do the work and document it, offline when they need to.

  • Stewards

    Run the garden: plan the season, welcome members, review work.

  • Evaluators

    Set the baseline and what good evidence looks like.

  • Funders

    See delivery before and after they give.

  • Community members

    Witness the work and confirm what was done for them.

No one confirms their own work.

5 / 16

We connected promises to Cosmo-Local service vouchers.

Cosmo-Local Credit is the open voucher protocol from Grassroots Economics. With it, members and gardens offer their own services and trade access in one shared pool.

Issue

A member or a garden offers a service as vouchers, like an hour of help or a hub visit.

Earn or exchange

Kept promises earn vouchers. Members can also swap them in one shared pool.

Book

Booking holds the vouchers in escrow until the service happens.

Confirm

Delivered units go back to the issuer. Anything undelivered returns to the holder.

Rehearsed end to end. The live deployment is under way.

Work

In the pilot, a designer, an analyst and an engineer build a website together. Each confirmed part goes into their portfolio.

Learning

Vouchers earned on projects book career calls and one-to-one help.

Tools

Vouchers open hub visits and AI tool seats members can't afford alone.

Each promise is confirmed by the person it served, so the record carries weight with the next employer, mentor or funder.
