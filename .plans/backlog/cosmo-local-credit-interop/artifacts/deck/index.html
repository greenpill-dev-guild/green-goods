Five roles keep a garden going, from gardener to community member.

  • Gardeners

    Do the work and document it, offline when they need to.

  • Stewards

    Run the garden: plan the season, welcome members, review work.

  • Evaluators

    Set the baseline and what good evidence looks like.

  • Funders

    See delivery before and after they give.

  • Community members

    Witness the work and confirm what was done for them.

A garden is a community doing regenerative work together in one place: a solar hub, a waste crew, a farm or a class.

9 / 17

We built commitment pooling: promises a community can see, keep and fund.

The hub asks for what it needs: the climate course completed, workshops run, discussions led.

The hub offers what it has: desks with power and internet, as hub-visit vouchers.

Members offer or ask too, like a career call. Kept promises earn agreed rewards and service vouchers, swapped in one shared pool.

Vouchers run on Cosmo-Local Credit from Grassroots Economics. Deployed on public networks and in pilot with Tech and Sun; rehearsed end to end with demo accounts.

Keep promises, and doors open: proof, power and people.

Proof

In the pilot, a designer, an analyst and an engineer build a website together. Each confirmed part goes into a portfolio the next employer can read.

Power

Vouchers open the hub's desks, with steady power and internet, and AI tool seats members can't afford alone.

People

Vouchers book career calls and one-to-one help, from members first, then from the diaspora.

11 / 17

Capital flows four ways: a shared fund, an endowment, the commitment pool and certificates.

Shared fund: a supporter donates directly to the garden's shared fund, which the garden draws on for its work.

Endowment: a supporter's gift stays invested, and its earnings, typically 2 to 7% a year and not guaranteed, go to the work.

Commitment pool: kept promises earn agreed rewards and build the record funders read.

Impact certificates: a garden turns a season's impact into a certificate. Next, supporters can buy a share.
