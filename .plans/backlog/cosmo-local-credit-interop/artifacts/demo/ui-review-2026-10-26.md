# Clickable demo UI review · 26 October 2026

Where a first-time judge would hesitate on each screen of the [clickable demo](README.md). Findings
only: nothing in the app changed. Each line names the screen and where it came from: **App** is the
develop app or dashboard run against the hub's record, **Storybook** a story id on the deployed
Storybook, **Composite** a Storybook dialog drawn over the App screen before it. The screens carry
Tech and Sun's content with every name changed (README, What is real and what is sample); the
wording around it is the app's own. Where the demo shows an invented handle, the app shows a
member's name when they have one and a shortened address when they do not.

## Top five

1. **The request hides its reward.** Tech and Sun's request "Complete the UN CC:Learn climate
   course" carries a $20 reward, but neither the group page nor the take-up sheet shows it
   (`PromiseGroupPage.tsx` and `TakeUpOneSheet.tsx` render no reward), so a member promises without
   seeing what keeping it earns (promise-4, promise-5).
2. **A course promise is confirmed in the words for help and work.** The requester's page says "The
   work is in" over "1 certificates" (promise-10), and the confirm sheet asks "Did the help
   arrive?" with "Confirm the Help Arrived" (promise-11): `selectConfirmCast` files every support
   request under "request", whose copy assumes delivered help.
3. **The season's 24 identical requests arrive as 24 rows.** The Promises sheet repeats "Complete
   the UN CC:Learn climate..." with "Asked for" and the same due date on every row (promise-3),
   although a group row exists (`PromiseGroupRow`, used by the group page's story set).
4. **Logging work meets an error-like dead end and a doubled unit.** After choosing Workshop
   Delivered, the Promise section says "No eligible promises match this garden and action."
   (work-4), the common case for work done for no promise; the next form labels a field "Delivered
   Duration (min) (min)" next to "Time Spent (hours)" (work-7).
5. **A funder has to finish the page to find the button, then meets token words.** "Support This
   Garden" sits at the very end of the hub's public page (funding-1), and the Endow dialog asks for
   a choice between "WETH, Wrapped Ether" and "DAI, Stablecoin" and to "Connect Wallet" (funding-3).

## Needs

- needs-1 · App, dashboard Hub, Assessments tab
  - The page subtitle "Review and triage pending submissions." describes the Work tab while the
    Assessments tab is open, and "1 waiting over a week" has no subject.
  - "Create Assessment", "Create Hypercert" and "Submit Work" are three equal outline buttons, so
    nothing marks the next step, and "Hypercert" is a term a judge will not know.
  - The dashboard's blue workspace tone (tabs, step numbers, Next) reads as a different product from
    the green member app and public site.
- needs-2 · App, Create Assessment, step 1
  - The step rail truncates its labels and hints ("Actions & Reporting ...", "The challenge, what
    you'll measure, and how...").
  - Nothing on the step says who will read the assessment or that it joins the hub's record.
- needs-3 · App, step 2
  - The outcome example under an Education assessment is a solar one ("e.g., Local technicians
    maintain the solar systems on their own").
  - "SMART outcomes" and "Pick the closest fit (Cynefin)" name frameworks a steward may not know.
  - The Metric menu cuts its own choice short ("Sessions delivered (sessio").
- needs-4 · App, step 3
  - Every action shows its code under its name ("edu.deliver_session"), a developer identifier.
  - The period's help text promises work "aggregated into a verifiable impact certificate", a term
    the steward meets here for the first time.
- needs-5 · App, step 4, review
  - "Records this assessment on-chain" and "Your wallet will ask you once." are the only words about
    what Submit does; both are system words.
  - "It can't be changed once it's submitted." is small gray text in a gray box, easy to miss before
    a step that cannot be undone.
- needs-6 · Composite: `admin-workflows-assessment-reviewstep--in-flow-sent` over needs-5
  - "Your assessment has been recorded." does not say where members or funders can now see it.
  - The disabled Edit buttons stay on screen as gray pills, which look broken rather than finished.
- needs-7 · Composite: Storybook `client-home-gardensheets--insights-with-banner-at-390` over the
  hub's Home
  - The assessment card shows only its title, dates and domain; the targets the steward set do not
    appear.

## Promise

- promise-1 · App, `/home` with TAS HUB chosen
  - The four header buttons (wallet, promises, work, notifications) are icons with badges and no
    labels.
  - The season card says "0 of 1 kept · 99 days left" while that one promise waits only for the
    steward, so the hub's best news reads as a shortfall.
  - Members without a name appear as shortened addresses under their work (the demo shows invented
    handles).
- promise-2 · App, the season sheet
  - "Live now 24 promises" counts requests nobody has taken up as live promises.
  - "Seasons and campaigns keep separate counts. They're never added together." explains a rule the
    sheet has not raised.
- promise-3 · App, the Promises sheet from "Show Its Promises"
  - Twenty-four rows repeat one truncated title, chip and date (top five).
  - The tabs read "Live", "History" and "To confirm"; a member cannot tell which list holds what they
    can take up.
- promise-4 · Storybook `client-commitments-promisegrouppage--open`
  - The reward is missing (top five).
  - "You can hold up to 2 promises from this pool at once." uses "pool", the system's word.
  - Unlike the promise page, no season or hub line sits under the title.
- promise-5 · Storybook `client-commitments-takeuponesheet--ready`
  - The title says "Take up one of these promises" above a single summary card, so "these" points
    at nothing on screen, and the reward is missing here too.
  - "The app picks which one. It becomes yours alone, and it is confirmed on its own." describes
    system behavior rather than what happens to the member next.
- promise-6 · Storybook `client-commitments-gardencommitment--in-progress`
  - "In progress" and "Needs you" sit side by side as two chips of equal weight.
  - "The lead chooses who joins" heads the team card of a promise one person keeps alone.
  - Copy-address icons beside each name are a developer affordance on a member's screen.
- promise-7 · Storybook `client-commitments-proofcomposer--media-added`
  - The gallery, camera and microphone buttons are icons with no visible labels.
- promise-8 · Storybook `client-commitments-proofcomposer--details`
  - "Only people on the team can be credited. The first proof that names someone gives them their
    share of the recognition." is a rule to parse before a single tap.
  - "Optional: you already added 1 photo." sits under the text box like an error.
- promise-9 · Storybook `client-commitments-proofcomposer--review-send`
  - The review crops the landscape photo of the course result into a narrow portrait tile, so the
    grade is cut off.
  - "You'll be asked to sign twice: once for the proof, once to send it." is the only line about
    what sending does.
- promise-10 · App, `/home/<TAS HUB>/commitments/19` as the requester
  - "1 certificates" (plural error) and "The work is in" for a course (top five).
  - The proof, the reason for the visit, sits below the fold under the team card.
- promise-11 · App, the same page's confirm sheet
  - "Did the help arrive?" and "Confirm the Help Arrived" (top five).
  - "Request · chiamaka provides. You asked for this, so you confirm it." breaks into three lines,
    with the name in monospace.
  - "Any one of the named people may confirm" lists two other stewards, but the summary above says
    "you confirm it", so who may confirm is unclear.
  - The proof photo is a small thumbnail; the grade cannot be read without opening it.
- promise-12 · Storybook `client-commitments-gardencommitment--kept`
  - The kept promise's only team row still reads "No approved work yet · 1 proof", which sounds
    unfinished under a green Kept banner.

## Work

- work-1 · App, `/home` with TAS HUB chosen
  - Nothing on Home says how to log work except "Start new work from the Garden tab", in small print
    under "Nothing to continue".
- work-2 · App, Garden tab (`/home/garden`), Start step
  - The Solar and Education tabs look like a table header rather than a choice; Solar is chosen by
    default with no hint that it was chosen for you.
  - Each action card shows only "1 photo required"; nothing says what the action records.
- work-3 · App, Education chosen
  - Workshop Delivered is the fourth card of a sideways carousel; only one and a half cards show at
    a time, with no count or arrows.
- work-4 · App, Workshop Delivered chosen
  - "No eligible promises match this garden and action." (top five) sits under "Choose the promise
    and exact requirement this work fulfills.", a choice the screen then says does not exist.
- work-5 · App, Media step
  - Requirement chips are title-cased mid-phrase ("Session In Progress Or Materials", "Participants
    (Privacy-Safe)").
  - "0 of 1 photo" is the only sign that one photo is required; the disabled Details button gives no
    reason.
  - The gallery, camera and microphone buttons are icons with no visible labels.
- work-6 · App, three photos added
  - Each photo has only a remove button; there is no way to see which requirement a photo meets.
- work-7 · App, Details step
  - "Delivered Duration (min) (min)" (top five), beside "Time Spent (hours)": two durations in two
    units with no hint of how they differ.
  - The Topics Covered list is all Web3 subjects (Web3 basics, Smart contracts, DeFi and five more);
    a climate course fits only "Other".
  - "Feedback" labels what is really the work's description; its placeholder asks for "Notes about
    engagement, challenges, or feedback received".
- work-8 · App, Review step
  - The Details row "Action" carries an external-link icon, so it reads as a link out of the app.
- work-9 · App, the steward's Home after the upload
  - The new entry says "Pending" like the steward's own submissions; nothing says it waits for
    their review.
- work-10 · Storybook `client-work-reviewwork--evaluate-example`
  - Approve and Reject are pinned on screen while the work's details sit below the fold, so a
    reviewer can decide before reading them.
- work-11 · Storybook `client-work-reviewwork--feedback-example`
  - Low, Medium and High confidence have no hint of what each changes.
  - The feedback sheet covers the photos the steward is judging.
- work-12 · App, `/gardens/tas-hub`, approved submissions
  - Cards credit "Garden participant" in monospace instead of a name.
  - The hub's two Workshop Delivered cards sit apart, and the second has no description.

## Funding

- funding-1 · App, `/gardens/tas-hub`, the end of the page
  - "Support This Garden" appears only here, after the record, the promises, an empty certificates
    section and the stewards (top five).
  - The stewards appear as bare ENS chips with no roles or photos (the demo shows invented
    handles).
  - "Support This Garden", "View Public Evidence" and "Discuss Funding" read as three equal choices.
- funding-2 · App, `/fund`, opened from "Support This Garden"
  - Donate and Endow sit side by side with no hint on the card of how they differ.
  - Each card shows its endowment as token amounts ("0.2 DAI · 0.25 ETH").
- funding-3 · App, the Endow dialog, 500 typed
  - The amount is in dollars, but nothing shows how dollars become the chosen token (top five).
  - "Connect Wallet" is the only way on; there is no card path.
- funding-4 · Composite: Storybook `public-documentation-funding--endow` over funding-3
  - The wallet line at the foot ("Wallet … · Manage") is small and far from the amount it pays.
- funding-5 · Composite: Storybook `public-documentation-funding--endowed` over funding-3
  - "Endowed $500.00 to TAS HUB" offers "Endow Again" and "Close", but nothing about where the
    yield goes or how to follow it.
- funding-6 · App, `/gardens/tas-hub`, approved submissions
  - The section's honest line, "Approval records a local review; it does not by itself measure
    environmental outcomes.", is small gray text under the heading.
