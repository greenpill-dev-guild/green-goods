# Clickable demo UI review · 26 October 2026

Where a first-time judge would hesitate on each screen of the [clickable demo](README.md). Findings
only: nothing in the app changed. Each line names the screen, then the Storybook story id or the
live page it came from. The screens carry Tech and Sun's content (README, What is real and what is
sample); the wording around it is the app's own. Where the demo shows an invented handle, the app
shows a member's name when they have one and a shortened address when they do not.

## Top five

1. **The request hides its reward.** Tech and Sun's real request "Complete the UN CC:Learn climate
   course" carries a $20 reward, but neither the request page nor the take-up sheet shows it
   (`PromiseGroupPage.tsx` and `TakeUpOneSheet.tsx` render no reward), so a member promises without
   seeing what keeping it earns (promise-2, promise-3).
2. **System words at the moments that matter.** The assessment review says "Records this assessment
   on-chain" and "Your wallet will ask you once" (needs-3); the Endow dialog asks a funder to pick
   WETH or DAI and "Connect Wallet" (funding-3); members without a name appear as `0x5e...0b2`-style
   addresses on the Promises tab, the confirm sheet, the Work tab and the endowment panel.
3. **The confirm sheet cannot show what it asks you to confirm.** "2 pieces of proof are recorded
   but cannot be shown right now" sits above "Confirm the Work Was Done" (promise-5).
4. **A kept promise reads as unfinished.** Under the green Kept banner, both team members show "No
   approved work yet · no proof yet" (promise-6).
5. **The hub's public page undersells the record to a funder.** It opens on "Assessments 0" and
   "Certificates 0", keeps "Support This Garden" at the bottom of a long page, and the Fund list
   reached from the top bar puts TAS HUB last (funding-1, funding-2). The endowment panel then labels
   every position "Campaign endowment" and reads "1 shares" (funding-4).

## Needs

- needs-1 · `admin-workspaces-hub--assessments-tab`
  - The page subtitle "Review and triage pending submissions." describes the Work tab while the
    Assessments tab is open.
  - "0 waiting over a week" has no subject; a judge cannot tell what is waiting.
  - "Create Assessment", "Create Hypercert" and "Submit Work" are three equal outline buttons, so
    nothing marks the next step, and "Hypercert" is a term a judge will not know.
  - The steward dashboard's blue workspace tone (tabs, step numbers, Next, Submit) reads as a
    different product from the green member app and public site.
- needs-2 · `admin-workspaces-hub--create-assessment-route`
  - The step rail truncates its labels and hints ("Actions & Reporting ...", "The challenge, what
    you'll measure, and how...").
  - Nothing on the first step says who will read the assessment or that it joins the hub's record.
  - Headings mix Title Case ("Assessment Context", "Domain & Context") with sentence case hints.
- needs-3 · `admin-workflows-assessment-reviewstep--in-flow-ready`
  - "Records this assessment on-chain" and "Your wallet will ask you once." are the only words about
    what Submit does; both are system words.
  - "It can't be changed once it's submitted." is small gray text in a gray box, easy to miss before
    an irreversible step.
  - The review is a long two-column list with Submit far down in the corner; "What You'll Measure"
    and "How Predictable Is This Work?" (a Cynefin choice) need context to read.
- needs-4 · `admin-workflows-assessment-reviewstep--in-flow-sent`
  - "Your assessment has been recorded." does not say where members or funders can now see it.
  - The disabled Edit buttons stay on screen as gray pills, which look broken rather than finished.
  - After sending, focus moves to the whole review panel and draws a blue frame around it (seen
    before the capture cleared focus).
- needs-5 · `client-garden-assessments--education`
  - The card cuts the title to one line ("Climate and tech skills for..."), and a phone has no
    tooltip to read the rest.
  - The card shows only dates and the domain; nothing tells a member what the assessment found or
    asks of them.

## Promise

- promise-1 · `client-commitments-gardenpool--live`
  - Five status chips (Offered, Asked for, In progress, Waiting to be confirmed, Needs you) share
    one shape and differ mostly by color, so an offer and a request look alike.
  - The list's count reads "7 live" and its filter "Live", a word the pitch reserves for shipped
    features.
  - The floating + button covers the right edge of the last visible row, and long titles end in
    an ellipsis ("Complete the UN CC:Learn climate ...").
  - Once the list scrolls, the season card is gone and rows no longer say which season they belong
    to.
- promise-2 · `client-commitments-promisegrouppage--open`
  - The request's $20 reward is missing (top five).
  - "What each one asks" and "Each one is a separate promise" assume the reader knows this is a
    group of identical requests.
  - "You can hold up to 2 promises from this pool at once." uses "pool", the system's word.
  - Unlike the promise page, there is no season or hub line under the title.
- promise-3 · `client-commitments-takeuponesheet--ready`
  - The title says "Take up one of these promises" above a single summary card, so "these" has
    nothing to point to, and the reward is missing here too.
  - "The app picks which one. It becomes yours alone, and it is confirmed on its own." describes
    system behavior rather than what happens to the member next.
- promise-4 · `client-commitments-proofcomposer--details`
  - "Only people on the team can be credited. The first proof that names someone gives them their
    share of the recognition." is a rule to parse before a single tap.
  - People appear as monospace handles ("chiamaka", "uche"), styled like code.
  - "Optional: you already added 2 photos and 1 voice note." sits under the text box like an error.
- promise-5 · `client-commitments-confirmsheet--ask-garden-work`
  - "1 of 1 rows fully approved by the stewards" uses "rows", a spreadsheet word.
  - "2 pieces of proof are recorded but cannot be shown right now." leaves the person confirming
    with nothing to look at.
  - The same rule appears twice in one sheet ("Only the person it was made to can confirm it" at
    the top, "Whoever did the work cannot confirm it" at the bottom).
  - "Garden work" names the kind of promise, not the course the member promised.
- promise-6 · `client-commitments-gardencommitment--kept`
  - The Kept banner sits above team rows that each say "No approved work yet · no proof yet".
  - The confirmer is tagged Steward here, while the confirm sheet said the person who asked
    confirms, so who confirmed is unclear.
  - Copy-address icons beside each name are a developer affordance on a member's screen.

## Work

- work-1 · `client-work-submitwork--media-with-example-photo`
  - The gallery, camera and microphone buttons are icons with no visible labels.
- work-2 · `client-work-submitwork--review-with-example-photo`
  - The "Action" row carries an external-link icon, so it reads as a link out of the app.
  - "It stays on this phone until it lands." uses "lands" for uploading.
- work-3 · `client-garden-gardenwork--all-work`
  - A bare "6" sits above the list with no label.
  - Nothing on a row tells a steward which items wait for their review; "Pending" is the
    gardener's word, so the demo's caption has to say "As steward".
- work-4 · `client-work-reviewwork--evaluate-example`
  - Approve and Reject are pinned on screen while the work's details sit below the fold, so a
    reviewer can decide before reading them.
- work-5 · `client-work-reviewwork--feedback-example`
  - Low, Medium and High confidence have no hint of what each changes.
  - The feedback sheet covers the photo the steward is judging.
- work-6 · `client-work-workdashboard--completed-with-example-photo`
  - "Your work was reviewed · Feedback" does not show whether Feedback opens the steward's note.
  - The refresh icon beside "3 items" has no visible label.

## Funding

- funding-1 · `https://greengoods.app/gardens/tas-hub`
  - The first screen shows "Assessments 0" and "Certificates 0" next to the hub's real counts.
  - The stat labels are tiny uppercase monospace and hard to read at a glance.
  - The only way to support from the top of the page is the site-wide Fund link; "Support This
    Garden" is at the bottom.
- funding-2 · `https://greengoods.app/fund`
  - From the top bar, TAS HUB is the last card on a long page (the page's own "Support This
    Garden" link brings it to the top).
  - Each card shows its endowment as token amounts ("0.2 DAI · 0.25 ETH").
  - Donate and Endow sit side by side with no hint on the card of how they differ.
- funding-3 · live Endow dialog on `https://greengoods.app/fund`
  - A funder must choose between "WETH, Wrapped Ether" and "DAI, Stablecoin" before paying.
  - The amount is entered in dollars, but nothing shows how dollars become the chosen token.
  - "Connect Wallet" is the only action; there is no card path.
- funding-4 · `client-public-vault-vaultmanagepositionspanel--positions`
  - The panel opens on "Connected wallet" with the wallet's address.
  - Every position is labeled "Campaign endowment", even a garden's.
  - "Redeemable now: 1 shares" has a plural error and uses investment wording without context.
  - Values show only as tokens ("500 DAI") with no dollar value.
- funding-5 · `https://greengoods.app/gardens/tas-hub`, approved submissions
  - Cards credit "Garden participant" in monospace instead of a name.
  - Many cards share one title ("Hub Service Session"), so the grid reads as repeats, and one
    Workshop Delivered card has no description at all.
