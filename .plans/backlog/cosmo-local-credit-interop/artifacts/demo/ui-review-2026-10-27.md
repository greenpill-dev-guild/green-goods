# UI review · clickable demo v5 · 27 October 2026

What the 49 screens show about the product, read from develop at `19d8ebf8e` (with the swap,
#1356, and the steward's voucher outcomes, #1360) on the hub's record, the local Storybook built
from the same commit, and the public site. v4 read `731ddba12`; the top five were checked again on
the new captures, and the notes by chapter carry over from v4 where their screens did not change.
Each finding names its screen. The demo works around the first five; they are worth fixing before
more people walk the app.

## Top five

1. **Home lists no open promise for the hub (promise-1).** Take Part on the hub's Home says "No open
   promises right now" and Highlights shows no season, while the hub's Promises tab lists the open
   season "Climate and Tech Education" and its course requests (season-3). A member opening the app
   sees nothing to take up. Not diagnosed: the v3 capture of 26 October showed the season on Home,
   reading develop at `907664c07` and the hosted indexer `de3e5df`; v4 and v5 read `731ddba12` and
   `19d8ebf8e` on `0bf0e0f` (`de3e5df` now returns 404), so either side may have changed it.
2. **Payouts read as off (season-1, season-7, the Promises tab).** Pool Funding shows "Funding
   unavailable", "Settlement unavailable" and "Settlement obligations could not be read", and the
   seed review's reward reads "None", while the settlement switch is off on develop. The pitch shows
   payouts on; the app should too before anyone else walks it.
3. **The deployed Storybook renders nothing.** Every story on design.greengoods.app still fails to
   render (checked 27 October on the wallet's Vouchers story): its build has no
   `VITE_ENVIO_INDEXER_URL` since #1330 removed the default indexer. The Storybook build needs the
   variable (or Shared should not read it at import time in Storybook). This demo was captured from
   a local build instead.
4. **The Impact page shows read failures.** Its Promises section says "Not available right now"
   twice and "Some figures are unavailable right now." (above impact-2), checked on the latest
   develop. A visitor reads these as a broken record. The bootcamp's record (impact-3) now opens as
   an evidence record whose details read, where v4 said "Work details are not available right now."
5. **A steward's queue counts work only someone else can review (approval-1, approval-2).** The
   Hub's Work tab shows "1" and "1 waiting over a week", and the only item is the steward's own;
   its dialog then says "Independent review required". The count could leave out what the viewer
   cannot act on, or say why it waits.

## The swap (new in v5)

- **The quote and its review (swap-2, swap-3)** say what she spends, the least she receives, the
  fee split (pool 0, protocol 0.01), how long the quote holds and where the vouchers go, and that
  the pilot's 1:1 rate "doesn't make the services equal in value". A judge can follow it without
  help.
- **The receipt (swap-4)** says the swap "didn't book anything", but its only action is Back,
  although the offer she came from is known. "Review and Redeem" there would save her a step.
- **Her page after the swap (swap-5)** shows "You hold 10." under You give, so she can see she has
  enough before she agrees.
- **Her active booking (swap-7)**: the booking steps read well, but the Team card under them says
  "Green Goods · Lead · No approved work yet · no proof yet" and "Recognition follows the record",
  promise-team copy that reads oddly on a service she bought.
- **Green Goods' acceptance has no story.** Green Goods accepts bookings in its own pool, the
  protocol pool, whose Accept dialog carries the protocol pool's warning, and develop's
  `VoucherAskDialogs` stories render only the hub's pool. So the demo shows the hub steward
  accepting a hub day (vouchers-7) but cannot show Green Goods accepting the seat. A story with
  the protocol target would let it.
- **The journey stories draw the page inside a 16px padded frame.** A scrolled page then shows
  content above the app's top bar (promise-5, swap-7); the capture takes the padding out, and the
  stories could do the same.

## By chapter

**Needs.** The sign-in (needs-1) and the wizard (needs-3 to needs-6) read well. The Hub's empty
Assessments tab (seen in v3) explains assessments clearly; with one assessment (needs-2) the row
truncates its title at about 30 characters on a 1440 screen, where there is room for the whole.

**Season.**
- The season card says "1 promise · 1 kept" beside "23 promises are still live" (season-3): it
  counts the group as one promise and its copies as 23. One unit in both places would read better.
- The seed flow's How Much step opens scrolled to the middle once an action is added (season-5).
- Proof & Confirmation offers "lina.eth" as its example name.
- The review (season-7) puts the reward last, below the fold of a 900-high screen; it is the line
  a steward checks most.

**Promise.**
- Take Part's sheet (promise-2) lists requests with the same sprout icon as offers, while Home's row
  uses the request icon. This is from the fixture, so check the real rows.
- The group page (promise-3) and its take-up sheet (promise-4) show one reward, "$20.00, paid in G$
  after it's kept" (#1335); her promise once taken up (promise-5) shows two, 2 Tech and Sun vouchers
  and G$. Whether the group page can show a voucher reward is untested here: its stories carry
  none, so a member may learn about the vouchers only after taking a promise up.
- Her promise in progress (promise-5) says where each reward comes from (Tech and Sun's reserve,
  the hub's pool), that each arrives on its own with its own receipt, and that nothing is set aside
  until her work is confirmed.

**Work.**
- Start (work-1) and Review (work-3) carry the promise as a pinned card, and Review shows the garden
  as facts (#1335).
- The media story cannot remove its example photo or take an upload, so Media for a promise can only
  be shown with the fixture's one photo.

**Approval.**
- The review form (approval-2, approval-3) is clear: confidence, feedback, audio note, who it
  affects, Reject and Approve. Approve stays disabled until a confidence level is chosen, and says
  so.
- After approval (approval-4) the panel says only "This work has already been reviewed." It could
  say what approving did: here, that it kept Ifeoma's promise.

**Vouchers (in pilot).**
- The reward rows (vouchers-1) and the Service Access facts (vouchers-3, vouchers-4) answer what you
  get, what you give, who holds it and who acts next.
- "Set aside for you? Not yet" still shows after both rewards arrived (vouchers-1).
- The steward's acceptance (vouchers-7) lists the four checks the contract enforces before Accept.
- The wallet's Vouchers tab (vouchers-2, vouchers-8) has no story inside the wallet sheet, so the
  demo shows the tab's panel alone.

**Impact.** The evidence ledger filtered to Education (impact-2) leads with the hub's bootcamp and
reads well. The record (impact-3) names its garden, TAS HUB, as plain text rather than a link, so a
visitor cannot go from the record to the hub's page to support it. Its Record Details, once opened,
show the attestation's explorer address, which names the chain.

**Funding.** Fund lists the hub first with Donate and Endow (funding-2). The Donate dialog
(funding-3) explains that payment uses cryptocurrency before Connect Wallet; it lists WETH before
DAI, where the connected state lists DAI first (funding-4 is reordered to match).
