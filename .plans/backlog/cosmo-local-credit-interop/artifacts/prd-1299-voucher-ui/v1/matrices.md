## Screen notes

### M01 · Home (active garden)

- **Actor and account:** Gardener (buyer and voucher holder), Green Goods account.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Active garden from the device preference (PWA hub lock 8). Rows from the garden-scoped indexer queries.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Nothing. Entry screen.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Rows open their records. The wallet launcher's badge counts personal purchases and voucher setups that need the member.
- **Recovery and who:** Offline: the shell's strip overlays the header; nothing below it moves (DL-085 / PWA lock).
- **Contract and projection facts:** No new reads on Home. The wallet badge reads the member's purchase and escrow states (account-scoped, not garden-scoped).

| State | Kind | What it shows | Screen reader |
|---|---|---|---|
| `default` | default | Unchanged Home journal. Voucher terms appear only as a request's detail line ("Pays 120 G$ + 20 Sunroot vouchers"); unlike assets are never summed. | None (page load). |
| `needs` | pending | A personal purchase that needs the member raises a count on the Wallet launcher, not in Continue (which keeps drafts and uploads) and not in Promises (scoped to the active garden; the purchase lives in another garden's pool). | Badge label: "Wallet, 1 needs you". |
| `offline` | offline | Offline strip. Purchases and voucher actions never queue (see M04, M05, M14). | "You're offline" from the shell's live region. |

### M02 · Visiting a provider garden

- **Actor and account:** Gardener, visiting. Any account can view.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Opened from Explore or a link. Visiting never changes the active garden (PWA hub lock 6).
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Nothing.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Offer rows show how they can be paid: garden payment, the member's own G$, or vouchers (issuer and amount).
- **Recovery and who:** Loading keeps the list header's height with "Gathering…" (DL-056).
- **Contract and projection facts:** Offer payment terms: the opted-in personal G$ route flag on the Individual Offer (upgraded CommitmentPooling) and accepted-voucher terms (VoucherEntitlementModule terms record), both indexed per offer and revision.

### M04 · Pay with your G$: garden, details, consent

- **Actor and account:** Buyer (gardener) with their Green Goods account. The buyer garden is one where they hold a member role; the provider garden is not required.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Offer opted in and approval-gated; buyer garden has an active registered Celo Safe (settlement account). Evidence: indexed garden roles and settlement account registry.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Exact consent: provider, buyer garden, service recipient, terms revision, service units, price, refund destination and the top-up choice. Signed once on Arbitrum (claim with pledge).
- **Pending, success, failure:** Pending: account prompt. Success: request recorded with a pledge (funding Pledged). Failure: nothing recorded; inputs kept.
- **Recovery and who:** Rejected or changed terms: buyer retries; consent is never reused across revisions. Offline: the primary is disabled; nothing queues.
- **Contract and projection facts:** Upgraded CommitmentPooling claim records consent to the exact revision; upgraded SettlementModule records the pledge (buyer garden, expected net amount, immutable refund account, top-up consent). Token fee read live from canonical G$ before showing the gross.

### M05 · Send G$ on Celo (transaction review)

- **Actor and account:** Buyer with their Green Goods account on Celo (passkey smart account).
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Request recorded with a pledge. The destination is the buyer garden's registered Safe from the settlement account registry, never typed. Evidence: registry read plus live G$ fee read.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** One canonical G$ transfer: gross amount, token fee, net arrival, network fee and sponsorship, and a reference that binds it to the request.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Pending in the account; success shows the transfer hash; failure states never imply funds moved.
- **Recovery and who:** Uncertain result: look up the transfer by reference before allowing another send (buyer). Sponsorship failure: pay the fee or retry later (buyer). Nothing queues offline.
- **Contract and projection facts:** Celo canonical G$ transfer to the registered Safe. This screen proves only "payment sent"; deposit recording is a separate steward attestation (S04). Lookup reads Celo transfer logs by sender, Safe and amount window.

### M06 · Your purchase (status and recovery)

- **Actor and account:** Buyer reads; acts with the Green Goods account (send, withdraw, confirm). Buyer-garden stewards and the provider act elsewhere (S03 to S06).
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Indexed request, pledge/funding state, provider decision, payout plan and refund child, plus the provider's access report.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Send, send the difference, withdraw (before acceptance), agree to a new revision, confirm kept.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Each fact is its own ladder step: payment sent, steward-verified deposit, provider acceptance, funding applied, access, confirmation, payout. A refund branch replaces the last steps. Payout and refund are exclusive.
- **Recovery and who:** Refund failure: buyer-garden stewards requeue the same refund (no second payment). Late deposit: steward records then refunds. Underpaid: buyer sends the difference or withdraws; returning an unrecorded underpayment is a Safe action outside the record (dependency).
- **Contract and projection facts:** Funding states map to Pledged, DepositRecorded, Consumed, Closed, RefundQueued, Refunded, Withdrawn (settlement spec) extended for the buyer-garden route. Payout uses the existing payout plan (contributor consideration by recognition), not a lump sum to the provider Safe. Access status is a provider report from the external integration, never inferred from payment.

### M08 · Take up with vouchers: terms, accounts, deposit

- **Actor and account:** Source buyer (Green Goods account, Arbitrum) consents; voucher holder (Gnosis wallet) deposits; issuer authority (issuer Safe on Gnosis) approves the setup.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Offer revision current; holder balance sufficient; issuer not paused (policy). Evidence: terms record, Gnosis balance, issuer approval record.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Consent to the exact revision, voucher amount, remedy, acceptance window and cancellation rules (Arbitrum). Allowance and deposit of the exact amount into the escrow keyed to this promise (Gnosis).
- **Pending, success, failure:** Setup prepared, issuer approved, deposit confirmed, escrow evidence received. Each is its own step; a sent message is shown as sent, never as received.
- **Recovery and who:** Uncertain deposit: check the escrow before retrying (holder). Missing issuer approval: wait; the holder can cancel the setup. Wrong wallet: switch wallet or get vouchers.
- **Contract and projection facts:** Selected design: consent recorded before core acceptance (upgraded CommitmentPooling acceptance guard). Setup prepared by VoucherEntitlementModule; issuer approval and deposit on GnosisVoucherEscrow / GnosisVoucherAdapter; authenticated escrow evidence over CCIP to Arbitrum. Exact function names: confirm against the reconciliation handoff.

### M09 · Your voucher promise (holder's page)

- **Actor and account:** Voucher holder reads. Acts with the Green Goods account (agree again, cancel, report, review split) and, for retries, any account allowed to resend the same command.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Indexed escrow state (Gnosis), evidence receipt and activation acknowledgment (Arbitrum), acceptance state, deliveries with evidence, frozen allocation and per-leg status.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Cancel setup (before acceptance only), agree to a revised version, open the split review, retry a failed leg with unchanged amounts.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Source state, destination execution and acknowledgment are shown separately (sent, done on Gnosis, confirmed). Acceptance and setup abort resolve to one outcome.
- **Recovery and who:** Cutoff never refunds automatically; the holder cancels. Cancellation may lose the race to acceptance; the page shows which won. After acceptance, only the service remedy applies. A failed leg is retried with the same allocation by the holder or a steward.
- **Contract and projection facts:** GnosisVoucherEscrow: one escrow per promise; setup refund only before activation; one immutable final allocation; independent release and refund legs; terminal only after both settle. Discharged vouchers return to the issuer reserve for reuse within its budget (no burn). Arbitrum side: VoucherEntitlementModule / VoucherRedemptionModule record evidence, activation and freeze.

### M10 · Review and freeze the final split (irreversible)

- **Actor and account:** Voucher holder, as the promise's counterparty, with the Green Goods account. Issuer authority has already authorized the return portion.
- **Preconditions and evidence:** Promise accepted and active. Provider reported delivered units with evidence and proposed a split within the agreed remedy, with issuer authorization. Evidence: delivery records and the authorization record.
- **Reviews and authorizes:** Two explicit confirmations: delivered service units (3 of 10 lessons), and the full voucher allocation (40 to the issuer reserve, 60 back). Then one irreversible freeze.
- **Pending, success, failure:** Pending in the account; success freezes one allocation and starts two independent transfers; failure keeps the review open with inputs intact.
- **Recovery and who:** Not Yet routes to the existing not-yet / dispute path. After freeze, amounts and recipients are read-only; only retries of the same legs remain.
- **Contract and projection facts:** One immutable settlement decision per escrow: full discharge, full return or the approved partial split, with delivered + returned = presented. Holder approval is its own record; core Fulfilled, a swap receipt or returned vouchers never substitute for it. Selected design: VoucherRedemptionModule (Arbitrum) freezes; GnosisVoucherEscrow executes. Confirm the freezing account against the reconciliation handoff.
