## Domain and fixture decisions

- A single contributor receives the agreed total under canonical recognition, without changing
  group allocation policy. A six-decimal voucher can specify `1_000_000` units as full access to
  one fictional booked session. Real prices, durations and capacity are not inferred from examples.
- Voucher-only and voucher-plus-G$ requests name work, evidence and review explicitly. Optional G$
  is a separate monetary item with asset, recipient, funder, payment method and receipt. Preserve
  existing G$ settlement behavior and accepted obligations; no mixed service checkout is added.
- Define each fictional service's full-access/discount status, quantity, duration, validity, booking,
  activation and remedy. No career-call material, actual issuer approval or private identities enter Git.
- Accepted rewards do not reserve inventory. Show actual local reserve and finite allowance separately
  from accepted undelivered rewards, service obligations/capacity and pool holding limits.
- One shared pool has explicitly fictional listing, price, fee, inventory and limit configuration.
  An independent candidate is input A `2_044_986`, gross B `1_022_493`, pool fee `20_449`, protocol
  fee `2_044`, net B `1_000_000`, at A/B price indices `1_000_000`/`2_000_000` and existing fee rates.
  This arithmetic is a test hypothesis until real pinned execution verifies it, not a universal rate.
