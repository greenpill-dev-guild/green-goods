# Roll out the two voucher pools

The release adopts the live Green Goods exchange and adds one Tech and Sun (TAS) exchange on Gnosis. Review and merge the code first. The TAS pool, funding and offer activation are separate operator decisions. The current application pins still describe the live Green Goods pool.

1. **Approve the operating inputs.** Recheck the TAS Safe, its locked guard and steward roster. The last readback found two required signatures; the current preflight expects the earlier one-approval policy. Decide which policy to keep and review the matching preflight or governance change before proceeding. Confirm the Arbitrum authority that can approve TSV rewards separately from the Gnosis reserve Safe. Approve hub-day terms, capacity and remedies; choose the initial stock, rates, fees and caps for TAS and the independent Green Goods listings. Preserve Green Goods’ existing 24-hour governance delay. For member issuers, obtain each member’s signature on the exact draft and retain the reviewed HTTPS terms and approval evidence with their digests.
2. **Prepare and rehearse the exact deployment.** On the approved commit, fill the manifest with the dedicated deployer, nonce and reviewed terms. Run `release clc` preflight, plan and simulation on an isolated Gnosis fork, then run downstream simulation with the resulting plan. Review the existing GG adoption, six new TAS components, any approved member tokens, signer, nonce, native budget, bytecode and initializer parameters. Rerun planning if the commit, manifest, authority or nonce changes. A prediction or fork receipt cannot become a production address.
3. **Deploy and verify.** After the operator approves the reviewed packet for broadcast, use the downstream execution path. Save its journal and verify canonical receipts. If a send is uncertain, reconcile its exact intent and receipt before any next send. The verified release produces a new `deployments/100-garden-exchanges.json`, a separate current readback and a registration packet. Keep the historical pilot artifact intact.
4. **Configure and fund through the right owners.** Use separately reviewed Safe transactions to register and fund the selected listings and bind source reward authority, reserve allowance and escrow. Confirm finite inventory and bookable capacity. Green Goods admits only its own reviewed listings. A new career-call or subscription offer needs approved terms and real capacity before it appears as available.
5. **Roll out Agent, indexing and app pins.** Rehearse the Agent startup against a copy of its persistent SQLite database, then take a verified backup before the production rollout. Keep the durable award and draft history on that database, verify schema version 11, and smoke-test the signed `/public/service-issuer-desk` and `/public/voucher-awards` APIs, their browser origins and Gnosis reads. Add the canonical TAS address and start block to the Gnosis `CLCSwapPool` config in `packages/indexer/config.yaml`. Confirm indexing reaches admission and funding and that events stay scoped by pool. Add the verified pool and listing pins to the Shared catalog in a separate activation change, with the verified terms and readbacks, before promoting Client and Admin.
6. **Walk the live pilot before expanding access.** Use approved real accounts for request reward and direct hub booking, member exchange and booking, earned TSV to GGV and Green Goods booking, and one steward award. Compare the balances, booking and award receipts, exact Safe approval and indexed state after each step. Pause expansion if authority, receipt, stock, terms or indexing diverge. Preserve the journal and persistent database during recovery; already-confirmed transfers are not undone by an app rollback.

Preparation commands from repository root, after the manifest inputs are reviewed. The preflight also works on the draft and reports missing inputs. Planning and simulation must use the exact committed manifest and source.

```sh
bun run --cwd packages/contracts contracts -- release clc --mode preflight --manifest config/clc-garden-exchanges.json
bun run --cwd packages/contracts contracts -- release clc --mode plan --manifest config/clc-garden-exchanges.json
bun run --cwd packages/contracts contracts -- release clc --mode simulate --manifest config/clc-garden-exchanges.json --plan .generated/clc-release/green-goods-tas-garden-exchanges.plan.json
bun run --cwd packages/contracts contracts -- release downstream --mode simulate --manifest config/clc-garden-exchanges.json --plan .generated/clc-release/green-goods-tas-garden-exchanges.plan.json
```
