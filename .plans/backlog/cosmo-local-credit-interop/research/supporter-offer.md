# Sponsor a season at Tech and Sun

**One-page supporter offer and FAQ. First draft 2026-10-09; status updated 2026-10-20 and
2026-10-21.** First diaspora community: Omo Yoruba of Southern California (confirmed by Afo,
9 October). Every amount is a labeled scenario until the steward confirms terms (COM-46, by
21 October). The prepaid vouchers are a planned pilot: they go live only after a recorded go
(PRD-1197) that addresses every release gate, and none of those gates was met on 21 October. Two asks are kept apart on purpose:
service capacity at Tech and Sun, and Green Goods infrastructure.

## The offer in one line

Fund useful services close to home, and follow what your support makes possible. *(working line,
to test with one real supporter)*

## What you fund

A season of hub services at Tech and Sun in Awka: certification seats, weekly climate discussions,
workshops and a small repair reserve. The hub's draft season plan prices this at about $800
(ten certification seats at $30, twelve discussions at $10, five workshops at $40, and $180 for
member offers and repair). One seat is about $30. *Scenario: the steward sets final prices.*

## Who receives it, and how to give

Tech and Sun (registered as [registered NGO name, to insert]), to deliver named services to
named learners at the Awka hub.

- **Today, for a US supporter:** give through Omo Yoruba of Southern California, the 501(c)(3)
  that already acts as Tech and Sun's US fiscal sponsor, earmarked for this season pack. Omo
  Yoruba's treasurer confirms the method (check, bank transfer or the processor they name); the
  website's donate page carries no payment form yet. Green Goods records the funded seats as
  commitments and reports their delivery. *Accepted route (Afo, 9 October), subject to the sponsorship terms.*
- **Later, and only after a recorded go:** prepaid vouchers, such as a Tech and Sun voucher for
  one solar class seat, bought from the hub's Cosmo-Local pool. A steward admits the hub as an
  issuer with published terms, and its outstanding vouchers stay under a cap that grows with the
  promises it keeps.
- **Not this route:** the in-app Donate button sends support to a garden jar that members draw
  from in small claims. It is real and live, but it is not how a season of seats is paid for.

## What you get back

- A record of each service as it is delivered, confirmed by the person who received it, not by us.
- A running view of what is still owed: seats bought, seats used, seats remaining.
- A season summary you can share, with definitions, dates and sources on every figure.
- Honest status when something slips: a missed session is shown as missed until it is made up.

The record reads without a wallet or an account. It is evidence for your decision; it is not a
credit score, an audited impact claim or a financial return.

## What stays with the community

The hub owns its voucher, its terms, its prices and its Cosmo-Local pool. Members choose what is public about
them. Your support buys services and the evidence of their delivery; it does not buy a say over
the hub, its land or its people.

## Costs

- Transfer costs depend on the route you use. Sending $200 from the US to Nigeria averaged 2.72
  percent in Q3 2025 and £120 from the UK averaged 1.96 percent (World Bank). We do not claim to
  save you fees.
- Green Goods sponsors the gas for members' in-app actions; a supporter using the app pays no
  network fee today. Any provider fee on a card route would be shown before you pay (not available
  yet).
- No platform fee is charged on sponsorship in this pilot.

## If something fails

- The hub cannot deliver a session: it is honored on the next open day. For a seat funded today
  through Omo Yoruba, any other remedy follows the fiscal sponsorship terms. On the planned
  prepaid-voucher route, a remedy returns the voucher units under terms published with the voucher.
- A learner does not confirm: the service shows as unconfirmed, with the fallback path visible.
- A payment does not arrive or arrives twice: the record shows the stopped state and who owns the
  repair.
- Green Goods vouchers have no cash-out. Tech and Sun's cash swap-back is a rehearsal idea
  only, pending its own terms and legal review.

## FAQ

**Is this a donation?** A gift through Omo Yoruba of Southern California is a donation to a US
501(c)(3), earmarked for Tech and Sun's season under its fiscal sponsorship terms. Whether it is
deductible for you depends on those terms and your own position. Support sent in-app is not a
charitable donation.

**Is a voucher money?** No. A voucher is a claim on one issuer's future service under published
terms, named with its issuer and unit, such as a Tech and Sun voucher for one solar class seat. It
is not a wage, a score or cash.

**How is a voucher used?** Presenting it opens a promise, with the person who presented it as the
counterparty. That person confirms when the service is delivered, and the voucher is discharged
once. This is the planned design; the rehearsal uses a stand-in service desk.

**Can I pick the learner?** Not in the first season. The hub allocates seats under its membership
rules; you see the aggregate and, where a learner consents, their confirmation.

**What if I want to give time instead of money?** Career calls and work introductions are the
services members asked for most. A diaspora professional can offer them as promises in the same
record, confirmed by the person helped.

**Can I get my money back?** Direct support is final. A prepaid voucher that the hub cannot honor
is returned as an unused voucher, not as cash, unless the published terms say otherwise.

**Who checks the hub?** The people served confirm each service. Green Goods publishes the record
and its definitions. Nobody has audited the outcomes; the record shows what happened, not what it
caused.

**Is this live?** The record of promises and G$ support are live. The Cosmo-Local pools and the
public passport are a planned pilot: no voucher contract exists yet, and nothing goes live before a
recorded go that addresses every release gate. We label rehearsal data as rehearsal.

**What does Green Goods get?** Nothing from the service budget. Infrastructure is funded
separately and we say so.

**What about my data?** The record shows aggregates and consented items only. No member is ranked.
No wallet addresses are published.

**What happens after the season?** You get the season summary and a plain next step: renew, adjust
or stop. Your choice.
