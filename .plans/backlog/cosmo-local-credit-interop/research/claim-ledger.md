**Vocabulary (decided 21 October).** The instrument is a *voucher*, named with its issuer and
unit, for example "Tech and Sun voucher, one solar class seat"; terms and docs may say *service
voucher* at first mention. "Credit", "token" and "points" stay off it, and "redeem" keeps its vault
meaning, so a voucher is *used*. The verbs earn, buy, swap and use and the phrase "voucher
exchange" are proposals, not decisions; this package says "Cosmo-Local pool" for the partner's
pool. Earlier drafts called the voucher a credit. *Cosmo-Local Credit* stays the protocol's family
name.
