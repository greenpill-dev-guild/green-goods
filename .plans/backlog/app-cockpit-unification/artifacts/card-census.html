Status and titles

Where the status pill sits decides how much room a title gets. Today the pill shares the title's line, so a long title, or a long status word in Spanish or Portuguese, gets cut off. Each column draws the same five cards with the status in a different place, at a phone's width, and counts the titles that get cut off. Switch the text to Long, Español or Português to push them.

Every card type

Every card type built from the four shapes, with your current settings, one phone wide. Titles take one line, so every row keeps the same height; a title that runs out ends in an ellipsis and shows in full when you point at it. Two lines stay available for each type. A detail that doesn't fit drops out whole, last first.

The detail line follows the five rules agreed on 18 October. Switch Detail line to Before to see the line as it was.

  1. Each card type orders its details by importance, so the least useful drops first.
  2. The status and at most two details. A third fact belongs on the item's own page.
  3. Nothing the card already shows: the icon says offer or request, the chip says a person's role, the chrome names the garden.
  4. Short ages and dates in every language, and an age is always a number.
  5. A row opens its item when tapped. An action stays only on the row that needs you, below the text.
