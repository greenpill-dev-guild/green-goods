The four shapes

Shown at the app's density

Nearly every card in the census is one of these. Each lives once in Shared, at two densities, and every object's card is built from them. The dials in the card tweaker change all four at once, and its live preview shows each change as you make it.

Work

3 designs in use · 1 shared row nothing renders

On a phone the app shows work as a row: a photo, the title, a status pill and a coloured edge. Its upload queue uses a different card. The cockpit shows the same work as a card with a photo grid on top. Shared also has a workbench row with the right fields that nothing renders.

Proposed Mock

Stays with work: the upload-queue states (saved, uploading, blocked), the photo count, and the photo grid on the cockpit's desktop card.

Commitments

5 designs in the app · 2 rows in a cockpit pool card

The app gives promises five separate designs: a row, a group row, the pinned promise in Submit Work, a queued act and a pending creation. The cockpit lists them as rows inside its Offers and Requests card. The shared status badge is the only piece both use.

Today Storybook

Also in the code, without a story: PendingCreationRow (app, 196 lines, a promise still being created); CommitmentPeople (app, 196 lines, labelled facts; the cockpit has a 42-line component of the same name that does a different job).

Proposed Mock

Stays with commitments: the act phase line (waiting on the wallet, confirming, done, queued, failed), the queue and retry states, and who a promise is between.

Card primitives

5 today · 1 proposed

Five components draw a card's surface. The app has its own Card, the cockpit has AdminCard, and Shared has three that no app renders. The proposal keeps one card surface, the app's: a 24px corner, a visible edge and a faint shadow. The card corner and edge dials above set it.

Today Storybook

Also in the code, without a story: Card (app, 97 lines, rendered in 13 files).

The proposed cards are a mock drawn from the design tokens, not the shipped components. They settle direction and proportion, not exact sizes. Example names, gardens and amounts are invented. A pick locks only when a before and after pair of the real components is approved.
