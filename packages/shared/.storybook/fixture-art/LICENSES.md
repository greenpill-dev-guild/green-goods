# Fixture photos

Storybook's fictional gardens and work use the photos in this folder. A photo illustrates a
fictional record; it is not evidence of that record. None of them shows a person.

## Photos from Wikimedia Commons

These nine are public domain or [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/), so
they can be redistributed with this repository. Each was downloaded from its Commons page on
2026-10-25, cropped where noted, resized to 480 px on its long side, and saved as a JPEG with no
metadata. Nothing else was changed.
