The member

Join, do one useful thing, get confirmed, use a service, come back.

  1. LiveJoin the hub with a passkey. No wallet words, no gas prompts.
  2. LiveTake up a useful request: host the weekly climate discussion.
  3. LiveThe people who came confirm it. The record shows who confirmed and how.
  4. LiveRecognition for the work, and G$ support when the promise is kept.
  5. PlannedUse a hub service with a credit: a certification seat or a workshop.
  6. PlannedConfirm the service. The hub's obligation is discharged once.

No chain names on a member's screen. "Saved on this phone" and "Sent" are different states. A retry never duplicates a payment.

Planned pilot · rehearsal by 21 October · decision 22 October

Hub services as credits: earned or purchased, within what the hub can deliver.

What a credit is

A claim on one issuer's future service under published terms. Not money, not a wage, not a score.

Two issuers

Tech and Sun and Green Goods each own their credit, terms, prices and pool, through a community Safe.

No cash-out

Green Goods credit has none. Tech and Sun's swap-back is a rehearsal idea pending its own terms and legal review.

Appendix B · Who can do what

Community-owned instances, bounded authority, named gates.

PartyCanCannot
Community Safe (Gnosis)own the credit, registry, limiter, fee policy, pool and issuer; move pool money; change signers; upgrade after a delaybe replaced by a Green Goods key
Steward hat (Arbitrum)authorize issuance within the cap and limit changes by signed instructionhold a Gnosis key or move pool money
Issuer (Gnosis)write to the credit and limiter up to the season cap; reject replays and unknown senderswithdraw pool money; burn a holder's credit
Green Goodsown the Arbitrum sender and its own pool; deploy Tech and Sun's instanceskeep any key to Tech and Sun's instances

Gates before live pools

  • External audit
  • 3-of-5 Safe ownership
  • 48-hour timelock
  • Two weeks of testnet operation
  • Tested rollback
  • An explicit human go; any substitution named and accepted individually

Code caveats today: a generic Fulfilled status can include fallback confirmation; yield has exactly three destinations; the token's burn is owner-only on its own balance. The 500-unit holding limit is a cap, not a loan or proof of capacity.

Appendix D · Definitions

Say the smaller, exact thing.

Commitmentwho promised what, with evidence and confirmation; not spendable money
Confirmationthe person served says it happened; fallbacks are visible
Credita claim on one issuer's future service under published terms
Presentmenthanding a credit to the issuer to ask for the service
Dischargethe record that a delivered service cannot be claimed again
Passporta readable account of promises, funding, delivery and obligations
Poolthe hub's exchange inventory under published rates and limits; never a Safe

Say

  • confirmed by the person served
  • within what the hub can deliver
  • the record is proof, not money
  • built to work with Cosmo-Local
  • rehearsal, labeled

Do not say

  • guaranteed principal, passive income
  • scam-free, fee savings
  • audited impact, institutional approval
  • equity, land or carbon from a certificate
  • a Safe called a pool
